A boundary for one task

Every one of the 512 nine-bit predecessor windows was checked. This covers the complete local causal cone on the infinite line for the next central output. The winning view is sufficient for that one-step prediction. It has no certified autonomous update rule.

The search chooses from 20 supplied features: nearby bits, adjacent XORs, one side-to-side XOR, three past bits and three temporal XORs. The minimum is within that grammar. This is a concrete test of observation selection; it does not discover an unrestricted biological object.
