## The question

The [thirteenth unit](2026-09-11-ring-closure-certificate.md) certified closure, refinement depth `h_* = 0`, at every ring from a sixteen-vertex pair graph. Depth itself had only been censused ring by ring on rings 6 to 12, where units eight to ten found it ring-dependent. This unit extends the certificate one step. `h_* ≤ 1` at ring `n` means two steps of observed history determine the third, so pairs of configurations agreeing on `ψ` and on `ψ ∘ F_r` are the closed walks of length `n` in a 256-vertex pair graph `G_{ψ,r}` (vertices four-cell pair blocks, edges five-cell pair blocks agreeing on both), a violating seven-cell block whose two-step observed successors disagree is a three-edge walk, and `r` fails depth one at ring `n ≥ 4` exactly when such a walk has a return walk of length `n − 3`. Boolean powers of the adjacency matrix repeat, so the depth-one set `D_ψ(n)` at every ring is decided by finitely many powers, subject to the frozen cap of 1024 powers per rule.

## Answer

- **L1, divisibility (theorem control).** `D_ψ(kn) ⊆ D_ψ(n)` held on every pair of exhaustive rings 3 to 14 for all eight observations.
- **L2, the criterion is exact (theorem control).** The graph criterion reproduces the exhaustive depth-one sets at every ring 4 to 14 for all eight observations, and the exhaustive sets at rings 6 to 12 equal the depth-at-most-one sets read from the recorded tables of units seven to ten.
- **L3, the certificates (reported).** No pair was censored: every one of the 2,048 matrices repeated well inside the cap, the largest first-repeat index being 33 (under 22) and the rule periods 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 12. Least eventual period `P_ψ` and onset ring `N_ψ`: 232 (1, 9), 4 (1, 9), 32 (1, 9), 200 (1, 10), 22 (3, 28), 102 (1, 5), 90 (4, 7), 150 (6, 7). All-ring depth-one sets have 118, 81, 81, 144, 115, 248, 130 and 98 rules.
- **L4, stabilization (bets a and b held, control c held).** Under 232, 4, 32 and 200 the depth-one set is the same at every ring from 10 on (in fact from 9, 9, 9 and 10): 118, 81, 81 and 144 rules. Under 22 the least eventual period is exactly 3, but it sets in only at ring 28: rings not divisible by 3 carry 183 rules from ring 19 on, while rings divisible by 3 carry 124 rules at 12, 15, 18, 21, 24 and 27 and 115 from ring 30 on, which is the all-ring set. Under 102 the depth-one set is the parity 248 at every ring from 5 on.
- **L5, the linear observations (theorem parts held; reported parts).** Rule 150 has depth one for all 256 rules on every ring not divisible by 3, and rule 90 gives the parity 248 on every odd ring from 5 on. Reported: on even rings, rule 90's depth-one set alternates forever between 130 rules (rings 8, 12, 16, …) and 138 rules (rings 6, 10, 14, …), so the sequence has period 4 in `n`; on multiples of 3, rule 150's set alternates between 100 rules (rings 9, 15, 21, …) and 98 (rings 12, 18, …), period 6, with 130 at ring 6. Neither is the 16 affine rules that closure gave: depth one is a much larger class than closure on the kernel-rich rings.
- **L6, symmetries (theorem controls).** Complement and reflection covariance held at every ring 4 to 14 for all eight observations.
- **L7, all-ring depth one equals full-shift depth one: failed.** The theorem direction holds (every full-shift depth-one rule has depth one at every ring), but the converse fails under 232 (16 rules: 43, 46, 58, 78, 92, 113, 114, 116, 139, 141, 142, 163, 177, 197, 209, 212), 200 (8 rules: 7, 21, 29, 71, 135, 149, 157, 199), 22 (22 rules) and 102 (8 rules: 41, 97, 107, 121, 134, 148, 158, 214), and holds under 4, 32, 90 and 150. For each listed rule some violating walk is bi-infinitely extendable in `G_{ψ,r}` (its start reachable from a cycle, its end reaching a cycle) but lies on no closed walk, so no periodic configuration realizes it: the ring-by-ring census, however complete, cannot see this depth-two behaviour of the full shift. Under 102 the eight rules `{41, 97, 107, 121, 134, 148, 158, 214}` are ones the sixth unit certified at depth exactly 1 on every ring; their full-shift depth is at least 2, which does not contradict that certificate, since it was a statement about rings. Post hoc, labeled: in the pair-agreement subshift of `G_{ψ,r}` (pairs agreeing on `ψ` and on `ψ ∘ F_r`), the periodic points do not reach every bi-infinitely extendable violating walk, so the periodic census is not complete for depth; this concerns that constraint graph, not density of periodic points in the ambient binary shift, and it is the first such gap in the program.

## Reading for the program

For the six observations audited on rings 6 to 12, the depth-one census was complete after ring 9 or 10 for four of them, and the ring-6-to-12 picture under 22 was not representative at all: its period-3 structure settles only at ring 28, beyond any ring the earlier units could enumerate, and the certificate is what makes that a fact rather than a guess. The linear observations show again that a kernel that varies with the ring produces a depth sequence that never settles, with the periods 4 and 6 of the two-step kernels rather than the 2 and 3 of closure. The L7 failure is the substantive new limit: for depth, unlike closure, the periodic configurations do not exhaust what the full shift does, so "at every ring" and "on the full shift" are different claims and must be stated separately from here on.

## Limits

Eight observations, exhaustive rings 3 to 14, certified rings `n ≥ 4`, depth at most one only; rules outside `D_ψ(n)` have depth at least two there and nothing more is certified about them. Depth two would need 4096-vertex graphs per rule. Nothing about observations outside the eight or about rings 1 and 2. No Class IV, novelty, renormalization or dimension claim.
