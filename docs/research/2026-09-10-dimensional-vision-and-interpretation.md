## The destination

Seek a recurring construction from one-dimensional CA to two-dimensional CA to three-dimensional CA and onward. Some trajectories should remain coherently representable through the tower, while each level admits additional native possibilities. The user calls the sought continuity a "turtle beam." "Unus mundus" is interpretive motivation, not an established mathematical or physical conclusion.

The central curiosity is what remains invariant through that tower, and what additional organization higher-dimensional states can support. An eventual program may itself participate in the unfolding: instruction changes generated by state evolution could expose a higher-order program. Ordinary determinism is already supplied by a deterministic update rule; the sought result concerns compatibility, representation, and organization across dimensions.

The user accepts incremental constructions and instruments. Guard and correction rows are valuable controls. Their prepared compatibility must remain visible as a resource, however, rather than silently serving as evidence that the organization arose naturally. Removing a guard is progress on one architectural constraint, not completion of the larger vision.

## Spatial dimension, memory, and description are separate choices

Adding another correction row within a plane does not create a new spatial dimension. Time, spatial dimension, correction depth, alphabet size, and neighborhood radius are distinct resources. Radius one is a current experimental choice, not a requirement of the vision. A wider radius, extra derivative or correction state, a different observation, or retained source information may each change what can close.

The [Rule32 correction cap](2026-09-10-rule32-physical-cap.md) and [guard-free axial family](2026-09-10-guard-free-axial-lift.md) therefore answer different questions. Neither erases the other. Matched local edits, globally changed programs, stored instructions, and changes to the effective data dynamics also require separate accounts of support and latency.

## A truth table supplies information, not its physical layout

For elementary CA, a state assigns a bit to a lattice position. A rule assigns an output bit to each of the eight possible three-cell neighborhoods. A state bit has a spatial address; a rule bit has a configuration address, such as101. The eight truth-table bits do not, by themselves, specify where their physical storage belongs.

The ECA model already supplies ordered left, center, and right input positions. That is real structure within the model. It does not uniquely assign positions to the eight output entries. Writing them as a binary integer, placing them along a row, or arranging them around a ring adds conventions. A rule becomes executable state through an encoding and an interpreter.

Consequently, keep three objects explicit: **the law, the state family, and the interpretation connecting them**. The interpretation may include a decoder, instruction placement, program/data organization, timing, and the fixed ambient interpreter. In the axial comparison it includes axis composition and literal replication, even though the rule word remains in the law.

Rearranging stored bits while compensating in the interpreter can preserve a computation. Rearranging them under a fixed interpreter can change the computation. Even a compensated rearrangement can alter locality, wiring, symmetry, or editing cost. An arbitrary global bijection is therefore not automatically an admissible physical equivalence.

Conversely, an invertible relabeling of an observation, with its update conjugated accordingly, preserves exact closure by algebra. That invariance alone is not new evidence. A substantive representation comparison must declare which changes are allowed and either compare different information retained by observations or account for different causal and resource constraints.

## What "resonance" could mean

The user suggested that some states might be more resonant with a rule than others. This is a useful question if the relationship is specified rather than treated as a single pre-existing score.

| Relationship | What must be established |
| --- | --- |
| Preservation | A family B satisfies F(B) contained in B, even if its individual states keep changing. |
| Faithful representation | An encoding E satisfies H composed with E equals E composed with F. |
| Restoration | Declared perturbations return toward the family or a specified represented trajectory, under an explicit metric and domain. |
| Endogenous organization | The relevant organization or interpretation is established or maintained by the unfolding, under declared initial resources. |

These properties do not imply one another in general. Restoration to some valid state differs from restoration of the original intended trajectory. Neither invariant geometry nor successful decoding proves attraction or spontaneous preparation.

There is already an exact example of state-dependent rule equivalence. In the [extension-freedom experiment](2026-09-10-extension-freedom.md), top rules128 and160 differ on neighborhood101, which the encoded top states never present. Both preserve the encoded evolution while acting differently elsewhere. More generally, F and H can agree on B; if their common evolution stays in B, that agreement persists through time. State organization determines which distinctions among instructions are consequential, while the instructions determine which configurations occur next.

This does not uniquely select an ambient law from a compatible image. Neutral endogenous instruction changes can also remain invisible to the represented data. Evolving instructions, changing their effective data semantics, and changing the overarching interpreter are distinct achievements.

## Refinement: preserving the beam versus preserving the old law

All256 axial laws preserve the property "unchanged along the added axis." In fact, a translation-equivariant map H preserves any configuration's translation symmetry: if tau(X)=X, then tau(H(X))=H(tau(X))=H(X). Thus a literal-replication family remains invariant under a uniform CA, even when the dynamics induced inside it are different from the desired source law.

The special66 rules in the axial census satisfy the stronger requirement that the induced dynamics equal the original lower-dimensional dynamics at the same macro cadence. For the other190, copied states remain copied but generally execute a modified law there. The earlier66 count must not be reported as an exhaustive count of rules preserving the copied-field symmetry.

Axis-permutation equivariance is another condition:24 sources have it,14 also satisfy exact replication intertwining. Reflection invariance was not classified. These precise distinctions should accompany future informal uses of "beam" and "resonance."

## Minimum dimension and the prime analogy

The user asks for the lowest dimension in which a state is representable, and whether states can skip levels. A fixed coherent tower of total adjacent embeddings cannot skip intermediate levels along its composed paths. For injective embeddings, the minimum ancestral level is preserved by lifting; compatible evolution can bring a state into an older image, so this ancestry cannot increase.

These statements concern the chosen embeddings. An intrinsic minimum representation dimension needs locality, decoding, dynamics, and resource constraints that prevent arbitrary coding from deciding the answer. There are additional local degrees of freedom as lattice boxes grow from length to area to volume; this is not a claim that the unrestricted infinite binary configuration sets have different cardinalities. Prime-like decomposition and uniqueness remain questions, not established arithmetic structure.

## Control refinement: persistent history, possible futures, and usable interventions

Further user clarification on 2026-09-10 adds a third axis: alongside which historical distinctions remain consequential and which futures are possible, ask which futures can deliberately be made to unfold. These are research questions, not demonstrated drives or optimization objectives of the current CA.

Persistence of information does not imply that it is affordably revisable. A set of possible outcomes is not an entropy until probabilities are supplied. A control quantity needs an action interface, a causal intervention model, an observation, a time horizon, and a resource budget. Ordinary action/outcome correlation alone does not establish intervention power.

Empowerment is a relevant information-theoretic control concept: channel capacity from actions to subsequent sensor states, optimized over action distributions under a stated model ([overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1863)). It is not control instead of information theory. Neither identifying a useful quantity nor measuring a channel supplies an endogenous controller.

For a dimensional lift L, evolution compatibility F_target L = L F_source does not automatically preserve cheap interventions. An action A needs a corresponding target action with A_target L = L A_source, together with an explicit cost comparison. Literal replication copies an edit along the new axis. Alternatively, native target edits may leave the inherited image and enable additional behavior; that is not automatically a failed lift.

The [loop checkpoint](2026-09-10-gradient-loop-invariants.md) supplies a conserved observation for eight source rules, under explicit periodic topology. The [next frozen intervention protocol](protocols/gradient-intervention-costs-20260910.md) will compare flat XOR edits, changed-edge costs, touched sites, and distinguishable terminal outcomes. It is unrun and assumes external simultaneous interventions on a known state. Sequential local implementation and changes driven by the unfolding program itself remain further questions.

The sharpened target is organization that persists under ordinary evolution while permitting affordable intentional revision. Do not treat stability, selectable futures, and a mechanism straddling them as already equivalent or already achieved.

## How experiments should advance the vision

Two complementary goals remain open: find relationships stable across admissible changes of representation, and explain how a particular interpretation becomes physically established. A constructor can supply a strong exact control without settling either goal.

Keep the existing frozen [transverse-difference protocol](protocols/transverse-difference-closure-20260910.md). It asks whether T(X)(x,y)=X(x,y) XOR X(x,y+1) evolves autonomously for the66 compatible sources. T=0 detects the replicated beam. Its fibers erase one baseline bit per column: equal T fields differ by a field constant along y. Whether those forgotten baselines affect the next differences is an exact closure question, relative to this observation. The test has not yet run when this perspective is recorded.

A failed radius budget is local evidence. Two complete states with identical full observations and different next observations provide a stronger obstruction to every deterministic update on that observation alone. Neither result condemns the full rule space. Use witnesses to motivate a separately frozen change of observation or additional state, rather than silently changing an unsuccessful experiment.
