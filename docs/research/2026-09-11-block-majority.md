## The observation

Rule 232, three-cell majority, applied once at cadence one. Unlike every observation in units five to seven it is not linear: its fibers are not cosets, it is non-surjective (image 53% of states at ring 6 falling to 26% at ring 12) and its fibers range from singletons to 98 states at ring 12. Closure of a rule means its successor's majority field is constant on every fiber; exact commutation with 232 is a sufficient condition (Research022's factor is then the rule itself on the image); `h_*` is the Research023 refinement depth by exact partition refinement.

## Answer

- **M1, commuters close (theorem control).** Every rule commuting exactly with 232 is closed at every ring 6 to 12, and the exact commuters are the same 14 rules at every ring: `{0, 4, 15, 23, 32, 51, 85, 170, 204, 223, 232, 240, 251, 255}`. The ten guaranteed by the protocol are among them (constants because majority fixes `0ⁿ` and `1ⁿ`, per the review's correction; identity and shifts; their complements by self-duality; 232 and its output-complement 23). Post hoc: rules 4 and 32 (an isolated one survives; a one between two ones survives) and their complements 223 and 251 also commute exactly.
- **M2, the remaining affine rules do not close.** None of `{60, 90, 102, 105, 150, 153, 165, 195}` is closed at any ring, scored by fiber-successor inequality. The pairwise witness holds for all eight at every ring: `0ⁿ` and a single isolated cell share the observation `0ⁿ`, and their observed successors differ. Majority, unlike every linear observation, does not preserve the affine rules.
- **M3, closed set versus commuters (reported).** The closed set is the same 22 rules at every ring: the 14 commuters plus `{2, 8, 16, 64, 191, 239, 247, 253}`. Those eight close by collapse rather than commutation: the factor of each is a constant on the image (all-zeros for 2, 8, 16, 64 and all-ones for their complements), because their outputs never contain two adjacent ones (respectively two adjacent zeros), so the successor's majority field is constant. This is immediate once seen: rule 2 fires only on the window `001`, and two adjacent firings would need one cell to be both 0 and 1.
- **M4, the factor is elementary on the image.** For all 22 closed rules the factor is radius-1 consistent on image words with every neighborhood seen at ring 12; for the 14 commuters it equals the rule itself on the image, and for the 8 collapse rules it is the constant table.
- **M5, history census.** `h_* = 0` exactly on the closed set at every ring. Reported, not predicted: the maximum depth is 2, 4, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 16 at rings 6 to 12, attained at ring 12 by `{106, 120, 169, 225}` alone, and 88 rules change depth across the rings. At ring 12 the histogram is 22 at depth 0, 96 at 1, 80 at 2, 26 at 3, 16 at 4, 4 at 6, 8 at 7 and 4 at 16. So under majority the memory cost of the contract is not bounded by a small constant within the tested rings and grows with the ring, in contrast to the two-step parity bound and the depth-4 ceiling of the richer linear kernels.
- **M6, symmetries.** Closed sets, commuter sets and depth tables are invariant under complement conjugation and reflection.
- **M7, relation to the parity criterion.** Neither inclusion holds. Rule 90 and 21 other constant-complement-response rules are not closed under majority, so `CLOSED_32 ⊄ C(n)` as frozen; and the 12 rules `{2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 191, 223, 239, 247, 251, 253}` close under majority without constant complement response, so `C(n) ⊄ CLOSED_32` (reported).

## Reading for the program

The first non-linear observation separates the rule space along two different mechanisms: exact commutation with the observation, and collapse of the observed successor to a constant. Neither has anything to do with the complement response that governed every linear kernel. For the primitives program this is the first observation under which an affine rule is not a safe primitive, and the first whose history cost grows with the ring size inside the tested range. The depth-16 rules `{106, 120, 169, 225}` are the natural targets for a frozen follow-up on whether that growth continues.

## Limits

One non-linear observation on rings 6 to 12. The 22-rule closed set and the 14 commuters are exhaustive there; the depth maxima and their growth are census facts with no proof and no claim beyond ring 12. The factor's locality is asserted on image words only. No Class IV, novelty, or renormalization claim.
