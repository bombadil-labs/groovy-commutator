## The observation

Rule 4 applied once at cadence one: a cell reads 1 exactly when it is an isolated one (window `010`). It is non-linear, non-surjective (image 28% of states at ring 6 falling to 8% at ring 12; no image state has two adjacent ones) and not self-dual, its complement conjugate being rule 223. The largest fiber, 853 states at ring 12, is the preimage of the all-zeros field and holds every state with no isolated one. Closure, exact commutation `K(n)`, the factor `B` on the image and the refinement depth `h_*` are as in the [eighth unit](2026-09-11-block-majority.md). A rule is a collapse rule, in `Z(n)`, when it is closed and its factor is constant on the image. The prediction N3 was that every closed rule commutes or collapses, `C(n) = K(n) ∪ Z(n)`, the shape found under majority.

## Answer

- **N1, commuters close (theorem control).** Every exact commuter is closed at every ring 6 to 12, and the commuters are the same 9 rules at every ring: `{0, 4, 42, 112, 170, 200, 204, 232, 240}`. The six guaranteed rules are among them; rule 255 is not a commuter, as frozen. Post hoc: the reflection pair 42 and 112 and rule 200 also commute. Rule 200 keeps exactly the ones that have a one-neighbor, so it sends every rule-4 image to `0ⁿ` and every rule-4 successor of its own output to `0ⁿ`.
- **N2, collapse without commuting (control).** Rule 255 is a collapse rule and not a commuter at every ring, so `C(n) ≠ K(n)` as frozen.
- **N3, commute-or-collapse: failed.** The closed set is the same 33 rules at every ring 6 to 12. The commuters are 9, the collapse rules 24 (`K ∩ Z = {0, 200}`), and two closed rules, **123 and 251**, are neither: their factor on the image is radius-1 consistent, not constant and not the rule itself, and it is the same table at every ring, the elementary rule 32 restricted to image words (output 1 exactly on the window `101`; the windows `011`, `110`, `111` never occur on the image). Every closed rule satisfies `π ∘ F_r = B ∘ π` by definition, so the class that is new here is not "closure through the observation" but "closed with a non-constant induced factor different from `F_r` on the image". For rule 251 there is a stronger explanatory mechanism, visible from the rule table: `251 = 255 − 4`, so `F_251 = ¬F_4 = ¬π`, and `π ∘ F_251 = (π ∘ ¬) ∘ π` because the rule itself is a function of the observed field. Rule 123 is **not** a function of the observation: `0ⁿ` and `1ⁿ` share the observation `0ⁿ`, yet `F_123(0ⁿ) = 1ⁿ` and `F_123(1ⁿ) = 0ⁿ`. What holds for 123 is the bounded identity that its observed successor agrees with rule 251's on every state of every ring 6 to 12, since both are closed with the same image-determined factor on the same image; that identity has no proof beyond the tested rings. Both rules are reflection-symmetric. This is the unit's finding: under a non-linear observation the closed set is not exhausted by exact commuters and collapse rules. Post hoc, unpredicted: under majority the analogous complement-of-the-observation rule `F_23 = ¬ ∘ F_232` is a commuter because majority is self-dual; independently, the eighth unit's exhaustive census found no rule in this non-constant-different-factor class there.
- **N4, the remaining affine rules do not close.** None of `{60, 90, 102, 105, 150, 153, 165, 195}` is closed at any ring, scored by fiber-successor inequality, and every frozen per-rule witness pair separates at every ring: `0ⁿ` against the block `11` for 60, 102, 150, 153, 195; against `111` for 105; against `110111` for 90 and 165. Per the review's caution, the `110111` block is the witness that works uniformly across rings 6 to 12 for 90 and 165 only; it also separates some other affine rules at some rings, and no per-ring exclusivity is claimed.
- **N5, history census.** `h_* = 0` exactly on the closed set. Reported: the maximum depth is 6, 6, 8, 14, 12, 12 and 22 at rings 6 to 12, attained by the reflection pair `{62, 118}` at every ring except 8, where `{107, 121}` attain it; 160 rules change depth across the rings. The maximum is not monotone in the ring size. At ring 12 the histogram is 33 at depth 0, 48 at 1, 24 at 2, 26 at 3, 34 at 4, 44 at 5, 8 at 6, 11 at 7, 10 at 8, 7 at 9, 7 at 10, 2 at 16 and 2 at 22. The eighth unit's maxima under majority were 2, 4, 3, 4, 7, 8, 16 at the same rings.
- **N6, symmetries.** The closed, commuter and collapse sets and the depth table are reflection-invariant. Complement conjugation is covariant, not invariant: `r` is closed, commuting, collapsing or of depth `h` under observation 4 exactly when `r̃` is so under observation 223, at every ring, while `C_4(n) ≠ C_223(n)` as sets at every ring. This is the program's first census that transports only when the observation is transported with the rule.
- **N7, relation to the earlier criteria.** `CLOSED_32 ⊄ C_4(n)` as frozen, through rule 90. Reported: 25 closed rules lie outside `CLOSED_32`; neither `C_4` nor the majority closed set `C_232` contains the other, their intersection being the 12 rules `{0, 4, 170, 191, 204, 232, 239, 240, 247, 251, 253, 255}`, and the two commuter sets meet in `{0, 4, 170, 204, 232, 240}`.

## Reading for the program

Closure under a non-linear observation is not a two-way split. Under majority every closed rule commuted or collapsed; under isolated-cell detection two rules close with a non-constant induced factor that is not the rule, and for one of them, 251, the reason is that the rule is built from the observation itself. Under majority the complement of the observation is itself a commuter, and the exhaustive census there found no rule of this class; the two facts are separate. For the primitives program the consequence is that "safe under an observation" has more than two algebraic reasons, and a learner that checks only commutation or only collapse will misclassify rules like 251 and 123. The complement result sharpens the program's thesis in the same direction: a property that fails to be invariant can still be exactly covariant once the contract, here the observation, is transported alongside the rule. Post hoc: every collapse factor is the all-zeros field, which is forced, since a constant factor must be a shift-invariant image state and `0ⁿ` is the only one.

## Limits

One non-linear observation on rings 6 to 12. The 33-rule closed set, the 9 commuters, the 24 collapse rules and the two rules with a non-constant factor that is not the rule are exhaustive there; the depth maxima are census facts with no proof and no claim beyond ring 12. That rule 123's observed successor matches rule 251's is exact on the tested rings only. The factor's locality is asserted on image words only. Nothing is claimed about the closed-set structure under other non-linear observations, and the identity between rule 123's and rule 251's observed successors is not claimed beyond ring 12. No Class IV, novelty, or renormalization claim.
