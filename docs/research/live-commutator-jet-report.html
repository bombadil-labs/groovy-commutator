Claude/Fable's period-four generator

Let a be a bi-infinite binary sequence containing neither 000 nor 111. Interleave zeros: X[2i]=a[i] and X[2i+1]=0. For Rule 54, H⁴X=X, and the two golden-growth equal-output source-pair components are represented by (X,H²X) and (HX,H³X).

(X,H²X) → (HX,H³X) → (H²X,X) → …
G(X)=0
G(HX)[2i]=0; G(HX)[2i+1]=a[i] XOR a[i+1]

Claude's independent review reproduced every numerical claim in PR #328 but also provided this much simpler temporal-half-period explanation. The review's tentative handwritten even-site value for G(HX) was corrected from 1 to 0. The domain-wall field d[i]=a[i] XOR a[i+1] forbids consecutive zeros and has golden-mean growth; knowing d determines a up to a single global polarity bit.
