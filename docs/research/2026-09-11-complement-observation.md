## Answer

- **Q2, the complement rule is in the residual class.** For all three observations and every ring, `¬ψ` neither commutes nor collapses, so it lies in `X`. The ring-10 pre-freeze check extended across the whole domain, as frozen.
- **Q3, the residual class lies in the complement rule's equivalence class: failed for rules 32 and 200, held for rule 22 at rings 7 to 12 and failed at ring 6.** Under rule 32, `X` is the same four rules `{33, 171, 223, 241}` at every ring; `¬32 = 223` is equivalent to the identity (rule 204), and 33, 171 and 241 are each equivalent to a commuter (`{32, 232}`, `170`, `240` respectively), not to 223. The theorem control, that the equivalence class of a closed rule consists of closed rules, held everywhere.

## Reading for the program

The construction half of the ninth unit's finding is general: for every non-self-dual observation tested, the complement of the observation closes and lands in the residual class. The equivalence half is not: the residual class is not the complement rule's orbit under observational equivalence, and its shape depends on the observation. Under rule 32 every residual rule is equivalent to a commuter, and under rule 4 so were 123 and 251 (to majority). Under rule 200 no residual rule is equivalent to a commuter, and sixteen of the seventeen have a factor that is not radius-1 consistent on image words, the first closed rules in the program whose factor on the image is not elementary. Under rule 22 the closed set itself depends on the ring, the first time in the program's non-linear censuses. Observational equivalence, by contrast, is exactly local: two rules produce the same observed successors on one ring of size at least 5 exactly when they do on all rings, and the word check turned the ninth unit's bounded identity into a theorem. For the planned primitives program, the safe statement is therefore: the complement rule always closes; for the three tested observations it also lies in the residual class; beyond that, residual membership requires separately establishing non-commutation and non-collapse, since for other elementary observations the complement rule can close by collapse (observation rules 2 and 16) or by commuting.

## Limits

Three observations on rings 6 to 12, six rules at rings 13 to 16. The closed sets, commuter, collapse and residual classes and the equivalence partitions are exhaustive there; the depth figures are census facts with no proof. The word-partition criterion is a theorem for `n ≥ 5` by the radius-2 lemma; nothing is claimed about rings below 5. The non-elementary factors under rule 200 are reported, not explained. No Class IV, novelty, or renormalization claim.
