## The emerging hierarchy

The research is now constructing a nested grammar of causal possibility:

\[
\text{generated states}
\to
\text{allowed adjacencies}
\to
\text{allowed 3-words}
\to\cdots
\]

Each level is a finite-state outer approximation to the true one-defect reachable language.

The exact residual counts so far are:

\[
5360
\xrightarrow{\text{edges}}
228
\xrightarrow{\text{3-words}}
170.
\]

The first spatial step is enormous. The second is smaller but unquestionably real.

That pattern suggests a useful distinction between two kinds of apparent causal possibility:

1. **local possibility:** states or short relations are individually allowed;
2. **composable possibility:** those local pieces can actually coexist inside a dynamically admissible longer context.

A representation that forgets composition can hallucinate causal futures that the dynamics cannot assemble.
