The hidden phase can stay hidden

New constructive result: Rule54's autonomous five-track commutator jet is executable by an explicit on-image nondeterministic symbolic transducer. Multiple internal source explanations may survive, but their next jet output is the same. This answers the preceding report's practical question without pretending the image is a full unrestricted 32-symbol shift.

We wondered / we tried / we found

Wondered: How do we actually update J5=(G,Q,R,A4,A5) without an astronomical off-image lookup table, given hidden preimages?

Tried: 16,384 length-14 source-context states and 32,768 length-15 source-word edges. Each edge reads a 5-bit J5 symbol and supplies the missing A6 bit. Existing exact full-line fiber closure makes the input/output relation functional on admissible entire jet configurations, though the automaton itself remains nondeterministic.

Found: A radius-six decoder filters a 13-symbol jet neighborhood through those state transitions and latches A6 at the middle. For every admissible window, the prior full-line locality proof forces a unique A6. Lower tracks update by the triangular commutator law:

G' = H(G) XOR Q          Q' = H(Q) XOR R
R' = H(R) XOR A4         A4' = H(A4) XOR A5
A5' = H(A5) XOR A6       [H = Rule54 per binary track]

This is a finite symbolic machine, not a minimized DFA or evidence of source compression.
