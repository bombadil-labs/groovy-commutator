## The question

The sixteenth unit decided full-shift refinement depth at most three under the four non-linear observations and deferred the two linear ones, 90 and 150, "because their depth sets vary with the ring". This unit is that deferral. Of the 20 rules outside `FS²_90` and the 34 outside `FS²_150`, which have full-shift depth exactly three and which at least four; is the rings-3-to-14 depth-three gap nonempty under each; and does the sixteenth unit's ladder picture — the full-shift depth class separated from the ring class at every level above closure — survive under an observation whose kernel, and therefore whose ring depth sets, change with the ring size.

The machinery is the sixteenth unit's, unchanged. The depth-three pair graph `G³_{ψ,r}` has the 65,536 eight-cell pair blocks as vertices, an edge per nine-cell pair block whose centre agrees on `ψ`, `ψ∘F_r`, `ψ∘F_r²`, `ψ∘F_r³`, and a violating three-edge walk per 11-cell pair block whose fifth observed successors disagree at the centre, 4,194,304 blocks per pair. `r ∈ FS³_ψ` iff no violating walk is bi-infinitely extendable, decided by strong components and two breadth-first searches on the sparse edge list. No matrix power is taken and no ring certificate at depth two or three is claimed. Three observations are run — 90, 150, and 90's complement conjugate 165, computed directly rather than deduced so that O6's covariance is a real regression; 150 is self-dual. Twelve rules are decided by pen before the run from the seventh unit's ring depths and are controls, not bets.

The monotonicity consequences held: `D³_90(n)` is all 256 rules at every odd ring in 3 to 14 and `D³_150(n)` at every ring not divisible by 3. The cross-implementation control held exactly: for every ring 6 to 12 and both observations, `D³_ψ(n)` equals the set of rules with `h_* ≤ 3` in the seventh unit's `h_star` table, which was computed by partition refinement over kernel cosets rather than by this unit's sorted-key enumeration. `FS³` has 250 rules under 90 and 246 under 150, against `FS²` of 236 and 222. The exhaustive sets are nearly saturated and move only on the rings where the kernel is nontrivial: `D³_90(n) = 256` except 254 at rings 10, 12 and 14; `D³_150(n) = 256` except 248 at ring 6, 250 at ring 9 and 246 at ring 12.
- **O2, the deep rules.** The control held: all twelve pen-decided rules (22, 151 under 90; 25, 37, 61, 67, 91, 103, 152, 188, 194, 230 under 150) lie outside `FS³`, each with an extendable violating walk in `G³_{ψ,r}` and each excluded from `D³_ψ(n)` on the rings the seventh unit's depth-4 entries predicted (22 and 151 at rings 10, 12, 14; the rule-150 ten at rings 6, 9 and 12 as applicable). The reported split, on which no bet was placed: of the remaining 18 rules outside `FS²_90`, 14 have full-shift depth exactly three and 4 have depth at least four (41, 97, 107, 121, with 200 violating walks each of which 4 are extendable); of the remaining 24 outside `FS²_150`, **all 24 have full-shift depth exactly three** and none is deeper. So under 150 the only rules beyond depth three are the ten the seventh unit had already decided by pen.
- **O3, the depth-three gap: the bet failed, under 150.** `Γ³_{90,[3,14]} = (⋂_{3≤n≤14} D³_90(n)) \ FS³_90` is nonempty — exactly the four rules 41, 97, 107, 121, each of which has depth at most three on every ring 3 to 14 and at least four on the line. But `Γ³_{150,[3,14]}` is **empty**: `FS³_150` and `⋂_{3≤n≤14} D³_150(n)` are the same 246 rules, so on rings 3 to 14 the periodic configurations already decide full-shift depth three exactly under 150. This is the frozen failure condition, and it is the first time in the program that a depth level closes rather than widens: the fifteenth unit found a depth-two gap of 12 rules under 150, and at depth three that gap is gone. The ring-14 gap `Γ³_{ψ,14}` is 4 rules under 90 and 10 under 150 — under 150 the whole complement of `FS³_150`, since `D³_150(14)` is all 256.
- **O4, the ladder: the bet held.** The class `c_FS = 3` is nonempty under both, with 14 rules under 90 and 24 under 150. Class sizes `c_FS = 0, 1, 2, 3, ≥4`: 90 (16, 114, 106, 14, 6); 150 (16, 82, 124, 24, 10). Class sizes `c_R`: 90 (16, 114, 112, 12, 2); 150 (16, 82, 136, 12, 10). Every one of these off-diagonal rules is a fifteenth-unit depth-two gap rule, and the fifteenth unit's six under 90 split 2 exactly-three and 4 at-least-four, while all twelve under 150 are exactly three. Under 150 the top class is entirely diagonal, `(≥4, ≥4)` with the ten pen-decided rules: the ladder under the self-dual linear observation agrees with the rings at closure, at depth one and at the top, and separates only at the single level the fifteenth unit exposed.

## Reading for the program

The sixteenth unit's reading was that the discrepancy between full-shift depth and ring depth "grows with the level rather than closing". Under the linear observations it does both, and which happens is a property of the observation, not of the level: under 90 a fresh set of four rules separates at depth three that did not separate at depth two, while under 150 the depth-two separation closes and rings 3 to 14 decide depth three exactly. So "periodic configurations do not decide depth" is not a uniform fact about depth; it is a fact about a pair (observation, level), and for one of the eight audited observations the rings catch up at level three. The kernel is the visible difference — 90's kernel alternates with the ring parity, 150's is nontrivial only on multiples of 3 — but this unit establishes the correlation, not a mechanism. The practical consequence for the program's verdict matrix is that a claim of the form "this property of the representation is decided by finite rings" must be stated per observation and per level, and cannot be inherited from a neighbouring level.

## Limits

Two observations and one conjugate, exhaustive rings 3 to 14, full-shift depth at most three only; rules in the top class have full-shift depth at least four and nothing more is certified about them, the twelve pen-decided rules included. No ring certificate at depth two or three, so both the nonempty gap under 90 and the **empty** gap under 150 are statements about rings 3 to 14, not about all rings: a larger ring could separate under 150, and nothing here forbids it. Ring 15, the next multiple of 3 for 150, was not computed, a scope choice that keeps `c_R` comparable with the fifteenth and sixteenth units. Nothing about the other six linear observations, about any observation beyond the eight audited, about depth four or beyond, or about rings 1 and 2. No relation between `FS^h_90` and `FS^h_102` is claimed although `ψ_90 = σ ∘ ψ_102 ∘ ψ_102`. No Class IV, novelty, renormalization or dimension claim. The canonical run took about 50 minutes; the CI replay timeout is 180 minutes, an engineering setting.
