## The question

The fourteenth unit certified refinement depth at most one at every ring and found 54 pairs `(ψ, r)` with depth at most one at every ring but not on the full shift. Two questions follow. How deep are those rules on the full shift? And does the rings-versus-full-shift gap recur at depth two? A ring certificate at depth two would need boolean powers of 4096 × 4096 matrices per rule; the full-shift question needs only reachability in a sparse 4096-vertex graph, so this unit decides full-shift depth at most two for all eight observations and all 256 rules, compares it with exhaustive depth two on rings 3 to 14, and turns the 54 abstract witnesses into explicit pairs of eventually periodic configurations.

The depth-two pair graph `G²_{ψ,r}` has the 4096 six-cell pair blocks as vertices and an edge per seven-cell pair block whose centre agrees on `ψ`, `ψ∘F_r` and `ψ∘F_r²`. A violating walk is a three-edge walk, a nine-cell pair block whose fourth observed successors disagree at the centre. `r` has `h_* ≤ 2` on the full shift iff no violating walk is bi-infinitely extendable: start reachable from a cycle, end reaching a cycle. The verifier decides this by strong components and two breadth-first searches on the sparse edge list; no matrix power is taken and no ring certificate at depth two is claimed.

## Answer

- **M1, full shift implies every ring (theorem control).** `FS²_ψ ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` held at every ring 3 to 14 for all eight observations.
- **M2, monotonicity and divisibility (theorem controls).** `FS¹_ψ ⊆ FS²_ψ`, `D¹_ψ(n) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` at every exhaustive ring, and `D²_ψ(kn) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` all held. The full-shift depth-two sets have 178, 97, 97, 172, 186, 256, 236 and 222 rules under 232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90 and 150, against full-shift depth-one sets of 102, 81, 81, 136, 93, 240, 130 and 98.
- **M3, the fourteenth unit's gap rules have full-shift depth exactly two: failed.** Of the 54 pairs, 30 lie in `FS²_ψ` and 24 do not: under 232 eight of sixteen (58, 78, 92, 114, 141, 163, 177, 197), under 200 four of eight (135, 149, 157, 199), under 22 twelve of twenty-two (140, 141, 143, 162, 163, 166, 176, 177, 180, 196, 197, 213), and under 102 none of eight. Each of the 24 has an extendable violating walk in `G²_{ψ,r}`, recorded with its endpoints' cycle and reachability facts; rule 58 under 232, for instance, has 592 violating walks of which 12 are extendable, the start lying in a 64-vertex strong component. So a rule can have depth at most one at every ring and depth at least three on the full shift. The 24 are exactly the all-ring depth-one rules outside `FS²_ψ`.
- **M4, parity is depth at most two on the full shift (theorem control by this unit's deduction).** `FS²_102` is all 256 rules, as the deduction in the protocol's Section 1 requires; the eight rules that the fourteenth unit found with all-ring depth one but full-shift depth above one all have full-shift depth exactly two.
- **M5, the depth-two gap: the bet failed.** The all-tested-ring gap `Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]} = (⋂_{3≤n≤14} D²_ψ(n)) \ FS²_ψ` is nonempty for every observation except parity: 20 rules under 232, 4 under 4 (9, 25, 65, 67), 4 under 32 (61, 103, 111, 125), 12 under 200, 30 under 22, 6 under 90 (41, 57, 97, 99, 107, 121) and 12 under 150 (44, 62, 74, 88, 100, 118, 131, 145, 173, 203, 217, 229). The bet named 232, 200 and 22 only; 4, 32, 90 and 150, which had no depth-one gap, acquire one at depth two. The ring-14 gap `Γ²_{ψ,14} = D²_ψ(14) \ FS²_ψ` equals the all-tested-ring gap under 232, 4, 32 and 200 and is larger under 22 (54 rules), 90 (12) and 150 (34), whose depth-two sets vary with the ring: under 90 every odd ring gives all 256 rules and the even rings 250, 244, 248, 242, 248 at 6 to 14; under 150 every ring off a multiple of 3 gives 256 and rings 6, 9, 12 give 244, 242, 234. Every gap rule is recorded with one extendable violating walk and its reachability facts. Neither set is an all-ring statement: `D²_ψ(n)` is known for rings 3 to 14 only.
- **M6, explicit witness pairs (control on the verifier).** All 54 fourteenth-unit witnesses were realized as eventually periodic pairs `(x, y)` by breadth-first search in the depth-one graph: a cycle, a path to the violating block, a path to a second cycle. Tail periods are 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 and the longest finite window has 38 cells. On every window `ψ(x) = ψ(y)` and `ψ(F_r x) = ψ(F_r y)` hold on the interior trimmed by three cells and `ψ(F_r² x)` differs from `ψ(F_r² y)` at the centre. The graph path is the certificate; the window replay is an implementation control, and it passed for all 54.
- **M7, symmetries (theorem controls).** `FS²` and every `D²_ψ(n)` are complement-covariant and reflection-covariant for all eight observations (the conjugate observations 223, 236, 251, 151, 165, 60 and 153 give the conjugate sets; 102's mirror is 60).

## Reading for the program

The fourteenth unit showed that depth, unlike closure, is not exhausted by periodic configurations. This unit shows the same gap one level up, and wider: four observations that had no depth-one gap have a depth-two gap, so the phenomenon is not a peculiarity of 232, 200, 22 and 102. It also shows that the gap can be deep. Twenty-four rules have depth at most one on every ring yet depth at least three on the full shift; the configurations that separate them from depth two can be taken periodic on each side of a finite defect yet lie on no single periodic configuration, and the explicit witness pairs make each such separation an inspectable object rather than a graph fact. The sparse method itself is the practical result: the full-shift question at depth `h` costs one reachability problem on `4^{2h+2}` vertices, while the ring question at the same depth would cost boolean powers of a matrix of that size.

## Limits

Eight observations, exhaustive rings 3 to 14, full-shift depth at most two only. Rules outside `FS²_ψ` have full-shift depth at least three and nothing more is certified about them. No ring certificate at depth two exists, so "all-ring depth two" is undecided and both gap sets are statements about rings 3 to 14. Nothing about observations outside the eight or about rings 1 and 2. No Class IV, novelty, renormalization or dimension claim.
