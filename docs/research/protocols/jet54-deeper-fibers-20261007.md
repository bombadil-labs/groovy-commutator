# Secondary frozen gate: how long does Rule 54 retain branching jet fibers?

**Date:** 2026-10-07. **Status:** frozen after the primary 54/30/90
comparison and before evaluating the two additional Rule-54 fiber graphs.
This is a *post-primary, prospectively scoped* follow-up and is not
confirmation of the original panel prediction.

## Motivation

The primary first-full-entropy GQR comparison found a structural contrast:
Rule 30 has only periodic recurrent non-diagonal equal-output source-pair
cycles; Rule 54 retains four strongly connected, branching non-diagonal
components even though GQR has full source spatial entropy.

Does Rule 54 remove the branching ambiguity when A4 and A5 are appended?

## Frozen domain

Exactly two additional Rule-54 G-anchored prefixes:

1. G,Q,R,A4 (level 4, source radius 5);
2. G,Q,R,A4,A5 (level 5, source radius 6).

Use the original panel's equal-output de Bruijn pair construction, pruning to
bi-infinite support and SCC classification.

For each prefix record:

- pair-edge count, essential vertices/edges;
- all recurrent non-diagonal SCCs;
- whether each is a simple directed cycle;
- SCC sizes, internal edge counts and Perron estimates;
- highest non-diagonal SCC spectral radius and its entropy;
- surviving periodic phase periods and XOR words;
- number of essential but transient off-diagonal interface states.

## Resource boundary

Stop as resource-censored if predicted equal-label paired edges exceed
5,000,000 or working memory would exceed 2 GiB. Do not widen scope or caps.
No Rule-30 or Rule-90 deeper pair graphs are authorized by this follow-up,
because their first-full-entropy outcomes were already decided.

## Interpretation

The original primary result remains unchanged. This unit only locates a
possible later transition from branching hidden ambiguity to periodic
phase-only ambiguity for Rule 54.

A phase-only transition at A4/A5 would not establish a universal
radius/phase-period correspondence or a Wolfram-class discriminator. Persistent
branching would not automatically imply positive *conditional* entropy for
each fixed observed jet configuration.

Deliver results separately as a clearly labeled secondary analysis in
results/jet54_deeper_fibers_20261007.json and the panel research note.
