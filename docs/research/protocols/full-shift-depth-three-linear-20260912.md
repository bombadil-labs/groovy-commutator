# Protocol: full-shift depth three under the linear observations 90 and 150 — 2026-09-12

**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), seventeenth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** gate 1 approved by an independent collaborating agent, OpenAI GPT-6 Astra Pro, on 2026-09-12 at protocol-only head `06265e7`; see Section 5.
**Why this unit:** the [sixteenth unit](../2026-09-12-full-shift-depth-three.md) decided full-shift refinement depth at most three for the four non-linear observations and omitted 90 and 150 "because their depth sets vary with the ring and belong to a separate unit" (its Section 1). This is that unit. The [fifteenth unit](../2026-09-11-full-shift-depth-two.md) already gives `FS²_90` (236 rules) and `FS²_150` (222), and found that both observations, "which had no depth-one gap, acquire one at depth two" (6 rules under 90, 12 under 150); the [seventh unit](../2026-09-11-linear-observations.md) found that "under rule 90 on even rings the maximum depth is 3 at rings 6 and 8 and 4 at rings 10 and 12 (rules 22 and 151 reach 4)" and "under rule 150 at rings 6, 9 and 12 the maximum depth is 4 at each ring", so some of the deep rules are already known by pen to have full-shift depth at least four. The question is the rest: of the 20 rules outside `FS²_90` and the 34 outside `FS²_150`, which have full-shift depth exactly three, which at least four, and is the sixteenth unit's ladder picture (diagonal only at closure) the same under an observation whose kernel varies with the ring. The sparse method of the fifteenth and sixteenth units is reused at the same depth `h = 3` (65,536 vertices) for three observations instead of seven, so this unit costs less than half of the sixteenth. Full-shift depth four (1,048,576 vertices, about sixteen times the per-pair cost) is not attempted here and is left for a separate decision.

## 1. Objects and definitions

Observations `ψ ∈ {90, 150}`, their complement conjugate `165 = T_c(90)` (150 is self-dual, `T_c(150) = 150`); rules `r ∈ 0..255`. Both observations are linear: `ψ_90(x)_i = x_{i-1} ⊕ x_{i+1}` and `ψ_150(x)_i = x_{i-1} ⊕ x_i ⊕ x_{i+1}`. On the ring their kernels are those of the seventh unit ("the complement pair, and for 90 on odd rings; the complement pair plus the two alternating patterns for 90 on even rings; and for 150, trivial unless `3 | n`, when it is the zero vector plus the three period-3 patterns and never contains `1ⁿ`"); on the full shift the kernel of 90 is the four period-2 configurations and the kernel of 150 the four period-3 configurations `x_{i+1} = x_{i-1} ⊕ x_i`. Nothing in the method uses linearity: the pair graph below is the sixteenth unit's, applied to these observations.

`h_* ≤ 3` at ring `n` means `P_4 = P_3`; `D³_ψ(n)` is computed exhaustively for `3 ≤ n ≤ 14` (five applications of the rule per state), the same rings as the fifteenth and sixteenth units, so that the ring classes below are comparable with theirs. Ring 15, the next multiple of 3 for observation 150, is not computed (see Section 4).

**The depth-three pair graph `G³_{ψ,r}`** is the sixteenth unit's, verbatim: vertices are the 65,536 eight-cell pair blocks; a nine-cell pair block is an edge from its first eight cells to its last eight exactly when its centre agrees on `ψ`, `ψ∘F_r`, `ψ∘F_r²` and `ψ∘F_r³` (radii 1 to 4); a violating walk is a three-edge walk reading an 11-cell pair block whose three nine-cell sub-blocks are edges and whose fifth observed successors disagree at the centre, `ψ(F_r⁴ x)_0 ≠ ψ(F_r⁴ y)_0` (radius 5); `4^{11} = 4,194,304` such blocks per pair. `r ∈ FS³_ψ` iff no violating walk is bi-infinitely extendable (start reachable from a cycle, end reaching a cycle), decided by strong components and two breadth-first searches on the sparse edge list; no matrix power is taken and no ring certificate at depth three is claimed.

**Historical sets and their transport.** The thirteenth to fifteenth units' result files carry `C_ψ`, `FS¹_ψ`, `D¹_ψ(n)`, `FS²_ψ`, `D²_ψ(n)` for 90 and 150 directly; they are read with their hashes. For 165 every historical set is **derived** by the sixteenth unit's transport rule `X_165 = {r̃ : r ∈ X_90}`, `r̃(a,b,c) = ¬r(¬a,¬b,¬c)`, by the complement covariance certified in the thirteenth to fifteenth units, and recorded as derived in the provenance; the `FS³_165`, `D³_165(n)` computed directly serve as the covariance regression in O6. The seventh unit's result file (`results/linear_observations_20260911.json`, its `h_star` tables for 90 and 150 on rings 6 to 12, computed by exact partition refinement over kernel cosets) is read with its hash for the consistency control in O1 and for the pen-decided part of O2; it is not otherwise used.

**Full-shift depth class and rings-3-to-14 depth class** are the sixteenth unit's definitions: `c_FS(r) = 0` if `r ∈ C_ψ` (closed on the full shift, thirteenth unit), `1` if `r ∈ FS¹_ψ \ C_ψ`, `2` if `r ∈ FS²_ψ \ FS¹_ψ`, `3` if `r ∈ FS³_ψ \ FS²_ψ`, `≥ 4` otherwise; `c_R(r)` the same from `⋂_{3≤n≤14} D^h_ψ(n)` for `h = 0, 1, 2, 3`, with `D⁰` the closed sets. Theorem: `c_FS(r) ≥ c_R(r)` for every rule. Under 90 and 150 the ring sets `D^h_ψ(n)` vary with `n` (period 2 and 3 for closure, 4 and 6 for depth one, certified by the thirteenth and fourteenth units; the depth-two sets vary on rings 3 to 14 without a certificate), so `c_R` is an intersection over rings with different kernels; this is stated, and the per-ring sets are reported alongside the intersection.

**Explicit witness pairs at depth two.** The fifteenth unit recorded, for each of its 18 rings-3-to-14 depth-two gap rules under 90 and 150 (41, 57, 97, 99, 107, 121 under 90; 44, 62, 74, 88, 100, 118, 131, 145, 173, 203, 217, 229 under 150), one extendable violating walk `(v_0, v_3)` in `G²_{ψ,r}` with its reachability facts, but did not realize them. Here each is realized by breadth-first search as an eventually periodic pair (left cycle, left path, violating nine-cell block, right path, right cycle), exactly as the sixteenth unit did for its 24 depth-two separations: the graph path is the certificate; a finite replay on three periods per side checks `ψ`, `ψ∘F`, `ψ∘F²` agreement on the interior trimmed by 4 cells and `ψ∘F³` disagreement at the centre, as an implementation control.

## 2. Frozen predictions

- **O1 (theorem and consistency controls).** `FS²_ψ ⊆ FS³_ψ`; `FS³_ψ ⊆ D³_ψ(n)` for every `3 ≤ n ≤ 14`; `D²_ψ(n) ⊆ D³_ψ(n)`; `D³_ψ(kn) ⊆ D³_ψ(n)`; `c_FS ≥ c_R` for every rule. Monotonicity consequences of the fifteenth unit's data, stated so that they are checked: `D³_90(n)` is all 256 rules at every odd `n` in 3 to 14 (on odd rings 90 has parity's fibers, and `D²_90(n) = 256` there), and `D³_150(n)` is all 256 at every `n` in 3 to 14 not divisible by 3 (150 is injective there). Consistency across implementations: for `n` in 6 to 12, `D³_ψ(n)` equals the set of rules with `h_* ≤ 3` in the seventh unit's `h_star` table for `ψ ∈ {90, 150}` (exhaustive sort against partition refinement).
- **O2 (the deep rules; pen-decided part and reported part).** Decided by pen before any run, from the seventh unit and the theorem `FS³_ψ ⊆ D³_ψ(n)`: rules 22 and 151 under 90 (`h_* = 4` at rings 10 and 12) and rules 25, 37, 61, 67, 91, 103, 152, 188, 194, 230 under 150 (`h_* = 4` at ring 12; the first six also at ring 9, the last four and 25, 61, 67, 103 also at ring 6) are not in `FS³_ψ`; the verifier must find an extendable violating walk for each of these twelve (control; a miss is an implementation error). Reported, no bet: the exact split of the remaining 18 rules outside `FS²_90` and 24 outside `FS²_150` into full-shift depth exactly three and at least four, each non-member with an extendable violating walk in `G³_{ψ,r}` and its reachability facts. No bet is placed on this split because the sixteenth unit's N2 direction (deep) was informed by four observations and there is no comparable prior for a kernel that varies with the ring.
- **O3 (the depth-three gap; bet).** `Γ³_{ψ,[3,14]} = (⋂_{3≤n≤14} D³_ψ(n)) \ FS³_ψ` is nonempty for each of 90 and 150. `Γ³_{ψ,14}` is reported without a bet (under 150 it is the whole complement of `FS³_150`, since `D³_150(14) = 256`). Neither is an all-ring statement. Failure: for some observation every rule with depth at most three at every ring 3 to 14 has full-shift depth at most three.
- **O4 (the ladder; reported, one bet).** The full cross-tabulation of `c_FS` against `c_R` for every rule under 90 and 150 (and 165) is reported. Bet: under each of 90 and 150 the class `c_FS = 3` is nonempty, that is, `FS³_ψ ≠ FS²_ψ`. Failure: some observation has `FS³_ψ = FS²_ψ`, every rule outside `FS²_ψ` having full-shift depth at least four.
- **O5 (explicit witness pairs; control on the verifier).** All 18 fifteenth-unit depth-two gap rules under 90 and 150 pass the finite replay. The finite data of every witness is recorded.
- **O6 (symmetries; theorem controls).** Complement covariance `FS³_165 = {r̃ : r ∈ FS³_90}` and `D³_165(n) = {r̃ : r ∈ D³_90(n)}`; complement invariance under the self-dual 150, `FS³_150 = {r̃ : r ∈ FS³_150}` and likewise for `D³_150(n)`; reflection invariance of every set under `r ↦ mirror(r)` (90 and 150 are reflection-symmetric).

## 4. Not claimed

- No ring certificate at depth two or three; `D²`, `D³` are known for rings 3 to 14 only, and every gap is a rings-3-to-14 statement. In particular nothing about the period of `D³_90(n)` in `n` on even rings or of `D³_150(n)` on multiples of 3: the fifteenth unit's depth-two sets on rings 6 to 14 do not visibly settle into a period, and this unit adds no certificate.
- Ring 15 is not computed, so `D³_150` is known on the multiples of 3 up to 12 only. This is a scope choice, not a technical limit: the ring range 3 to 14 is kept identical to the fifteenth and sixteenth units so that `c_R` means the same thing across the ladder tables (ring 15 would fit the sixteenth unit's 64-bit key, which packs `h + 1` observed fields of `n` bits; ring 16 would not).
- Nothing about full-shift depth four or beyond: a rule outside `FS³_ψ` has full-shift depth at least four and nothing more is certified; the twelve pen-decided rules in O2 are not thereby shown to have depth exactly four.
- Nothing about the other six linear observations (0, 60, 102, 170, 204, 240: trivial or complement-pair kernels, whose depth sets the seventh unit showed equal parity's or are trivial), or about any non-linear observation beyond the sixteenth unit; nothing about rings 1 and 2.
- No relation between `FS^h_90` and `FS^h_102` is claimed although `ψ_90 = σ ∘ ψ_102 ∘ ψ_102`; the sets are reported and may be compared post hoc, labeled.
- No Class IV, novelty, renormalization or dimension claim.
