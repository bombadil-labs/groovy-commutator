## Decision

Can Rules 18 and 126 forget source information at a strictly positive rate yet update their depth-two observations exactly? A confirmed result gives two test cases for the project's distinction between predictive sufficiency and reconstruction; a conflict or no proved loss blocks that interpretation. The simplest control is additive Rule 90. Restricted invariant domains and periodic rings are comparison data, not evidence for an unrestricted full-line claim.

- Source: [the #330 integration tree](https://github.com/bombadil-labs/groovy-commutator/tree/4883bd96975a45dec29274630f3b134e309ed3d6), especially `docs/research/2026-10-07-jet-full-line-autonomy.md` and its review addendum. The pilot tests a previously reported outcome, not a fresh blind prediction.
- Domain: one step of elementary CA Φ on the unrestricted binary line, with `A0 = I xor Φ`, `A(k+1) = Ak∘Φ xor Φ∘Ak`, observation `J2 = (A1,A2) = (G,Q)`. No centering, no restricted domain, no periodic approximations for the primary test.
- Autonomy: prove or refute that the next observed pair is a finite-radius function on the admissible current J2 image. Enumerate complete local windows or certify an equivalent language graph. A finite-radius witness on all windows proves the full-line claim; a finite-ring sample does not.
- Loss: under fair IID source bits, prove positive conditional entropy rate given the entire observed field. A finite invisible source substitution that embeds with positive probability in disjoint guarded blocks suffices. Exceptional infinite pairs or a finite-ring census alone do not.
- Scope: Rules 18 and 126 plus Rule 90 as control; no new census, deeper jets, or novelty claim solely from the result.

## Repeatable workflow

1. **Work:** Freeze this scope and the source commit. Preserve an executable, cheap certificate or a counterexample. Record a checkpoint if interrupted.
2. **Review:** An independent worker rederives the answer from the same source before seeing the implementation, then checks the exact evidence revision and its proof. Compare only after both passes. Record disagreements or unverified cases, not just successful runs.
3. **Close:** Publish a scoped synthesis with the verifier and the reviewer/commit in this main-based draft. If final wording depends on the integration draft, link its immutable revision instead of modifying it. Feed accepted results into Groovy's existing findings and knowledge process, not a second knowledge graph.
4. **Stop:** No automatic next science unit, scheduled Actions, merges or deployments. Stop after reviewed completion or one bounded recovery from interruption; do not retry a negative result. A future run can reuse the same packet format with a separately admitted, decision-changing question. The stopped Yang–Mills queue and the Wet Math Prediction Watch are untouched.
