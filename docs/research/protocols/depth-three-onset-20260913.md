# Protocol: the depth-three onset `N³_ψ`, certified at every ring by sparse boolean powers restricted to the carrying strong components — 2026-09-13

**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), twentieth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** pending gate 1 by an independent collaborating agent (Codex / OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol requested) on the gathering PR.
**Why this unit:** the [nineteenth unit's checkpoint](../checkpoints/representation-invariants.md) left four candidates and named the **depth-three onset** first: the one quantity its strong-component method cannot reach, "it needs the power sequence, so it inherits the eighteenth unit's cost question at 65,536 vertices and should be scoped before being frozen". It was scoped before this freeze by a throwaway probe (exploratory, not a result, no committed artifact; its numbers are recorded in Section 3 so the cost plan rests on measurements rather than an estimate). Two facts came out. First, the [eighteenth unit's](../2026-09-12-depth-two-certificate.md) cost objection to depth three was measured, not refuted: the depth-three pair graph has out-degree at most four exactly as the depth-two graph does (an edge extends a pair block by one of four pair cells, at every depth), but a packed power of the **full** 65,536-vertex matrix is `2^32` bits = **512 MiB** (the eighteenth unit's "128 MB" was an underestimate), and one such power costs **20.8 s** plus 1.65 s to hash — about 800 pairs times tens of powers is days, so the full-matrix certificate stays out of reach. Second, the full matrix is not needed. The ring criterion only ever reads `(A^{n−3})[v_3, v_0]` for a closed violating walk, whose ends lie in one strong component, and **every walk between two vertices of one strong component stays inside that component**; so the power sequence can be taken on the matrix restricted to the carrying components, whose sizes the [nineteenth unit](../2026-09-13-closed-violation-depth-three.md) already recorded: over its 790 pairs with a closed violation the union of carrying components has median 1,726 vertices and maximum **14,168** (rule 43 under 4, one period-1 component), never the 65,536. The probe measured **64 ms per packed power** at that maximum (24 MiB per power, 222 words per row) and a byte-confirmed certificate `(k, p) = (12, 1)` in 2.4 s; components of tens of vertices certify in milliseconds. The object of the unit is therefore the fourteenth and eighteenth units' `(k, p)` certificate, generalized from depth two to depth three exactly as the eighteenth unit generalized it from depth one to depth two, with one additional step (restriction to the carrying components, Section 1) whose correctness is a one-line lemma and is checked against unrestricted computation. The scientific question is the one the nineteenth unit stated as not claimed: between ring 14 and an undetermined onset, the per-ring depth-three sets `D³_ψ(n)` are unknown. The probe also showed the onset is not a formality — two rules under 232 (94 and 133, the two eventually-out rules the nineteenth unit found with no diagonal anchor) leave `D³_232(n)` at ring 9 and are **back in** at rings as late as 64, with onset 65 on a pair of 13-vertex components, more than double the depth-two record onset of 31 — so this unit has a genuine bet to lose.

## 1. Objects and definitions

Observations `ψ ∈ {232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90, 150}`, as in units thirteen to nineteen; rules `r ∈ 0..255`. `h_* ≤ 3` at ring `n` means `P_4 = P_3`; `D³_ψ(n) = {r : h_*(r, ψ, n) ≤ 3}`; `D³_ψ^∞ = ⋂_{n ≥ 4} D³_ψ(n)`. Historical data is **read with its hash, never recomputed**: `D³_ψ(n)` for `3 ≤ n ≤ 14`, `FS³_ψ` and the rings-3-to-14 gaps from `results/full_shift_depth_three_20260912.json` (232, 200, 22, 4 and conjugates; 32 by that unit's deduction `F_32 = F_4 ∘ ¬`) and `results/full_shift_depth_three_linear_20260912.json` (90, 150, 165); `D³_ψ^∞`, per-rule `|V³_{cl}|`, `n_min`, `p_r`, `R_r`, the carrying components with their sizes and periods, `P³_ψ` and the eventual sets `D³_{ψ,ev}(j)` from `results/closed_violation_depth_three_20260913.json`; the depth-two certificates, `D²_ψ(n)`, `P²_ψ`, `N²_ψ` from `results/depth_two_certificate_20260912.json`. `FS³_102 = D³_102(n) =` all 256 rules (fifteenth unit M4), so 102 is theorem-trivial at every ring and its onset is `N³_102 = 4`.

**The depth-three pair graph `G³_{ψ,r}`** is the sixteenth unit's `pair_graph(psi, r, 3)`, verbatim, imported from `scripts/verify_full_shift_depth_three.py`: vertices the `4^8 = 65,536` eight-cell pair blocks; an edge from a nine-cell pair block's first eight cells to its last exactly when its centre agrees on `ψ, ψ∘F_r, ψ∘F_r², ψ∘F_r³`; a **violating walk** is a three-edge walk reading an eleven-cell pair block whose `ψ∘F_r⁴` disagrees at the centre; `V³_{ψ,r}` its end pairs `(v_0, v_3)`; `V³_{cl}` those with both ends in one strong component. `A` is the adjacency matrix. **Out-degree is at most four** (an edge appends one of four pair cells; measured on the probe's five pairs: degrees take only the values 0, 2, 4, maximum 4, no vertex above 4).

**Ring criterion (theorem, thirteenth/fourteenth/eighteenth units).** For `n ≥ 4`: `r ∉ D³_ψ(n)` iff some `(v_0, v_3) ∈ V³_{ψ,r}` has `(A^{n−3})[v_3, v_0] = 1`; the eighteenth unit's pruning lemma and the nineteenth unit's Lemma A restrict this to `V³_{cl}` and give `D³_ψ^∞ = {r : V³_{cl} = ∅}`. A rule with `V³_{cl} = ∅` is **trivially certified** at every ring with no power computed.

**Restriction lemma (new here, pen; gate 1 is asked to check it).** Let `U` be the set of vertices of the strong components that contain an end of some closed violating walk (the **carrying components**), and let `A_U` be `A` restricted to `U` with only the edges internal to a single carrying component kept (the direct sum of the carrying components' adjacency matrices). For `(v_0, v_3) ∈ V³_{cl}` and every `m ≥ 0`, `(A^m)[v_3, v_0] = (A_U^m)[v_3, v_0]`. Proof: a walk `v_3 ⇝ v_0` of length `m` in `G³` has every vertex reachable from `v_3` and reaching `v_0`, hence in the strong component of `v_3 = ` that of `v_0`, so every edge of the walk is internal to that component and the walk exists in `A_U`; conversely every walk in `A_U` is a walk in `A`. ∎ Hence the bit sequence `b_m = [∃ (v_0, v_3) ∈ V³_{cl} : (A^m)[v_3, v_0] = 1]`, and with it `r ∈ D³_ψ(n) ⟺ b_{n−3} = 0`, is computed exactly from the powers of `A_U`. The number of vertices `|U|` is the nineteenth unit's recorded sum of carrying-component sizes (median 1,726, maximum 14,168 over its 790 pairs with a closed violation), read from its result and checked against the recomputed components.

**Sparse packed powers of `A_U`.** `A_U^m` is held as `|U|` rows of `⌈|U|/64⌉` unsigned 64-bit words; `A_U^0 = I`, `A_U^m[u, ·] = OR_{w ∈ out_U(u)} A_U^{m−1}[w, ·]`, at most four gathers and three ORs per power. This is exactly boolean matrix multiplication of the restricted matrix, no approximation. After each power `b_m` is recorded. **Exact repeat detection, the eighteenth unit's, unchanged:** the SHA-256 of each power's bytes is stored against its exponent; at the first exponent `k + p` whose hash already appears at exponent `k`, `A_U^k` is recomputed from `A_U^0` and compared byte for byte; if equal the certificate is `(k, p)`, least `k` then least `p` by the scan; a collision is recorded and the scan continues, so a collision delays and never falsifies. Then `b` is periodic from `k` with period `p`, and `r`'s membership in `D³_ψ(n)` is decided for every `n ≥ 4` by `b_1, …, b_{k+p−1}`. **The certificate `(k, p)` is a property of `A_U`, not of `A`**: it may differ from the `(k, p)` the full matrix would give (the full matrix carries transient structure outside `U`), and it is reported as the restricted certificate. Every derived quantity — `b`, the per-rule membership sequence, its eventual period and onset, the observation-level `N³_ψ`, `P³_ψ` and `D³_ψ(n)` — is a function of `b` alone and is the same under either matrix by the restriction lemma.

**Power cap and censoring contract (frozen, the fourteenth and eighteenth units', unchanged).** The scan stops at the first confirmed repeat or at `A_U^{1024}`; the cap is a verifier constant recorded in the artifact. A pair not repeated by the cap is **censored**: no `(k, p)` is claimed, its membership is decided only for `4 ≤ n ≤ 1027` and marked open beyond, and every observation-level quantity depending on it (`N³_ψ`, `P³_ψ`, the certified list) is recorded as censored, not inferred. No larger cap or rescue computation after the run.

**Onsets.** For a certified rule, its **membership sequence** is `m_r(n) = [r ∈ D³_ψ(n)]`, `n ≥ 4`; its least eventual period `p_r` is the least `q` with `b_{k+i} = b_{k+((i+q) mod p)}` for all `i` (the eighteenth unit's `rule_period`), and must equal the nineteenth unit's recorded `p_r` from cyclic classes (T1). Given the observation-level period `P`, the **rule onset** `N_r(P)` is the least `N ≥ 4` with `m_r(n + P) = m_r(n)` for all `n ≥ N` (the eighteenth unit's `rule_onset`: start at `max(4, k + 3)` and walk back while equality holds; the walk back is exact because beyond `k + 3` periodicity is certified). Trivially certified rules have `N_r = 4`. The **observation-level period** `P³_ψ = lcm_r p_r` (must equal the nineteenth unit's `P³_ψ`, T1) and the **onset** `N³_ψ = max_r N_r(P³_ψ)`, the least `N ≥ 4` from which `D³_ψ(n + P³_ψ) = D³_ψ(n)` for all `n ≥ N`. The verifier reports the certified list `D³_ψ(4), …, D³_ψ(N³_ψ + P³_ψ − 1)` and `D³_ψ^∞` as the intersection over one period from the onset (must equal the nineteenth unit's, T1). Divisibility (theorem, fifteenth unit): `D³_ψ(kn) ⊆ D³_ψ(n)`.

**Conjugates and mirrors.** Complement conjugation `r ↦ r̃`, `ψ ↦ ψ̃` and reflection act on `G³` as the vertex relabelings the nineteenth unit verified (complement `v ↦ v ⊕ (4^8 − 1)`; reflection reverses the eight cells, reverses walks, swaps the violating ends and transposes `A`). Carrying components map to carrying components, `A_U` to the conjugate's `A_U` (or its transpose), and the power sequence of a matrix and of its transpose have the same `(k, p)` and the same return-length sets, so certificate status, `(k, p)`, `b`, `N_r`, `N³_ψ`, `P³_ψ` and every `D³_ψ(n)` transport exactly (T4).

Output `results/depth_three_onset_20260913.json` with source hashes, parameters (cap 1024, sample sizes, vertex counts), per-pair status, restricted `(k, p)`, `|U|`, `N_r`, per-observation `N³_ψ`, `P³_ψ`, certified lists, `D³_ψ^∞`, and the T1–T4 verdicts with every violation; **no wall-clock field is serialized** (the nineteenth unit's #220 lesson; timing goes to stdout only). Registered in `scripts/check_result_integrity.py` once the canonical run exists. Note under `docs/research/2026-09-13-depth-three-onset.md`; the Program page's table gains a row; the `depth-two-certificate` and `closed-violation` knowledge entries gain the onset.

**Domain of computation at depth three.** Every rule with `V³_{cl} ≠ ∅` under 232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 90, 150 and under their conjugates 223, 251, 236, 151, 165 and the mirrors of the asymmetric observations' deep rules — the nineteenth unit's recorded domain (790 pairs with a closed violation among its 874 computed, the rest trivially certified); rules in `FS³_ψ` and rules with `V³_{cl} = ∅` are trivially certified by theorem and not powered. The graph is rebuilt for each pair (the violating-walk ends are needed exactly, and the graph is not stored), so the domain's graph cost is the nineteenth unit's.

**Cost, measured, and the CI-cost boundary.** Probe (this session, 4 cores, 15 GB, NumPy 2.4): depth-three graph construction 3.7–5.9 s per pair, as the sixteenth and nineteenth units measured; restricted packed power 64 ms at `|U| = 14,168` (24 MiB), negligible below a thousand vertices; certificates on the probe's twelve pairs needed 6 to 139 powers (confirmation recomputation included) and took 0.0–2.4 s. Full-matrix power, for context only: 512 MiB, 20.8 s, plus 1.65 s for SHA-256 — the reason restriction, not a bigger machine, is the method. Extrapolation: ~874 graphs at ~5 s is the nineteenth unit's 71 minutes; scans add at most a few seconds per pair unless a pair approaches the cap (1,024 powers at the maximum size is about 70 s plus confirmation, so even ten censored large pairs add under half an hour); T1(a) at depth two is the eighteenth unit's 35 minutes at most, likely less since `A_U` is smaller than the 4096-vertex matrix; T1(d)'s seven full-matrix pairs add about 20 minutes. **So the canonical run is expected at 100 to 150 minutes and bounded above by about four hours: it does not fit the ~10-minute automatic-CI budget of Myk's 2026-09-12 boundary and will be run once outside GitHub Actions from the pinned, reviewed implementation, with execution provenance recorded in the note and the result file, and reproduced by a full second execution (preferably in a separate environment, as the nineteenth unit did) for the determinism check.** Automatic CI on this unit's files runs only the seconds-scale `scripts/check_result_integrity.py` tier and the verifier's `--self-test` (the restriction lemma on synthetic graphs with known closed violations against explicit dense powers including a case with two carrying components and transient structure outside `U`; the repeat scan with a deliberately weakened hash; the depth-two restricted method on one observation against the eighteenth unit's certified list; under two minutes, writes nothing). The full byte-for-byte replay is a `workflow_dispatch`-only job requiring a non-empty authorization input, on the exact pattern of `research-closed-violation-depth-three.yml` (cheap tier with a 10-minute timeout on `pull_request`/`push`; `full-replay` only on manual dispatch with an authorization reference), created in the implementation sub-PR and never triggered automatically.
