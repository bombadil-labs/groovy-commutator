**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), tenth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** pending (Codex, gate 1, requested on the gathering PR before implementation).
**Why this unit:** the [ninth unit](../2026-09-11-isolated-cell.md) found that under isolated-cell detection two rules, 251 and 123, close with a non-constant factor that is not the rule. Rule 251 is `¬F_4`, the complement of the observation, so its observed successor is a function of the observation by construction; rule 123 only matched 251's observed successor on rings 6 to 12. This unit turns those two facts into predictions for three further observations: that the complement of the observation always closes and, for non-self-dual observations whose complement neither commutes nor collapses, always lands in the class with a non-constant factor different from the rule; that every rule in that class is observationally equivalent to the complement rule; and that observational equivalence is decided by five-cell windows, which certifies the rule-123 identity for every ring. A short annex extends the depth census of units eight and nine to rings 13 to 16 on the depth-record rules.

## 1. The transformations and their costs

Three observations, each an elementary rule applied once at cadence one on the `n`-ring, `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`: `ψ ∈ {32, 200, 22}`. Rule 32 reads 1 exactly on the window `101` (an isolated zero); rule 200 keeps a one exactly when it has a one-neighbor (windows `011`, `110`, `111`); rule 22 reads 1 on `001`, `010`, `100`. Definitional facts, from the rule tables and observation-only enumeration before freezing, not counted as results: all three are non-affine, reflection-symmetric and not self-dual, with complement conjugates 251, 236, 151; image fractions at ring 10 are 0.120, 0.271, 0.545; none fixes `1ⁿ` except 200, and all three fix `0ⁿ`. For each, the complement rule `¬ψ = 255 − ψ` (219 is not involved; the complements are 223, 55, 233) satisfies neither `ψ ∘ ¬ = ¬ ∘ ψ` on the image of `ψ` nor "`ψ ∘ ¬` constant on the image", checked from the observation alone at ring 10. Costs as in units eight and nine: forward radius 1, no inverse, fiber-dependent information loss, factor defined on the image only. The same census is run for the three conjugate observations `{251, 236, 151}` to score complement covariance.

Definitions carried from the ninth unit's protocol: closed set `C_ψ(n)`, exact commuters `K_ψ(n)`, collapse rules `Z_ψ(n)` (closed with a constant factor), refinement depth `h_*`. New: the **residual class** `X_ψ(n) = C_ψ(n) \ (K_ψ(n) ∪ Z_ψ(n))`, the closed rules whose factor is non-constant and not the rule; and **observational equivalence** `r ~_ψ s` iff `ψ ∘ F_r = ψ ∘ F_s` on every state of the `n`-ring. Lemma (stated, not a result): `ψ ∘ F_r` is a radius-2 local map, so for `n ≥ 5`, where every five-cell word occurs, `r ~_ψ s` holds iff the two radius-2 local tables agree on all 32 words, and the equivalence partition is the same for every `n ≥ 5`.

## 2. Frozen predictions

- **Q1 (the complement rule closes; theorem control).** For each `ψ` and every `n`, `¬ψ ∈ C_ψ(n)`, with factor `B = ψ ∘ ¬` restricted to the image. Proof: `ψ(F_{¬ψ} S) = ψ(¬ψ(S))` is a function of `ψ(S)`.
- **Q2 (the complement rule's class).** For each `ψ` and every `n`, `¬ψ ∉ K_ψ(n)`, `¬ψ ∉ Z_ψ(n)`, hence `¬ψ ∈ X_ψ(n)`. This follows from the Section 1 observation-only facts (commutation of `¬ψ` with `ψ` is exactly `ψ ∘ ¬ = ¬ ∘ ψ` on the image; collapse is exactly `ψ ∘ ¬` constant on the image) and is frozen as a check that the deduction survives the full census.
- **Q3 (the unit's question: the residual class is the equivalence class of the complement rule).** For each `ψ` and every `n`, every rule in `X_ψ(n)` is observationally equivalent to `¬ψ`: `X_ψ(n) ⊆ [¬ψ]_{~_ψ}`. A rule in `X_ψ(n)` whose observed successor map differs from that of `¬ψ` is a failure of Q3, listed with its factor table and its own equivalence class. Whether `[¬ψ]_~ ⊆ C_ψ(n)` (equivalence to a closed rule implies closure) is a theorem, since closure depends only on the observed successor map; it is checked as a control. The size of `[¬ψ]_~` and its members are reported.
- **Q4 (ring independence of observational equivalence; theorem control, and the rule-123 certificate).** For each `ψ ∈ {32, 200, 22, 4}`, the partition of the 256 rules by `~_ψ` computed by exhaustive enumeration is identical at every `n ∈ {6, …, 12}` and equals the partition by agreement of the radius-2 tables on the 32 five-cell words. In particular, for `ψ = 4`, rules 123 and 251 agree on all 32 words, which certifies `π ∘ F_123 = π ∘ F_251` for every ring `n ≥ 5` and closes the ninth unit's bounded identity.
- **Q5 (named commuters and collapses; theorem control).** For every `ψ` and `n`: `{0, 170, 204, 240, ψ} ⊆ K_ψ(n)` (rule 0 because every `ψ` fixes `0ⁿ`; identity and shifts; `ψ` itself). `255 ∈ K_200(n)` because rule 200 fixes `1ⁿ`; `255 ∈ Z_32(n)` and `255 ∈ Z_22(n)` because rules 32 and 22 send `1ⁿ` to `0ⁿ`, so `ψ ∘ F_255` is the constant `0ⁿ`. Constants commute only when the observation fixes the corresponding uniform configuration, per the eighth unit's binding correction.
- **Q6 (history census and depth annex).** `h_*` for all 256 rules at rings 6 to 12 under each `ψ`; `h_* = 0` exactly on `C_ψ(n)`; magnitudes reported. Annex, frozen as a bet: under majority (rule 232) the maximum of `h_*` over the ring-12 record rules `{106, 120, 169, 225}` at ring 16 exceeds 16, the ring-12 value. Under rule 4 the depths of `{62, 118}` at rings 13 to 16 are reported, with no prediction, since the ninth unit's maxima were not monotone. The annex computes only these six rules at rings 13 to 16.
- **Q7 (symmetries).** Reflection: `C`, `K`, `Z`, `X`, the equivalence partition and the depth table are invariant under `r ↦ mirror(r)` for each `ψ`, all three being reflection-symmetric. Complement: covariance, not invariance: `r` is in `C_ψ`, `K_ψ`, `Z_ψ`, `X_ψ` or has depth `h` exactly when `r̃` is so under the conjugate observation `ψ̃ ∈ {251, 236, 151}`, at every `n`; `[¬ψ]_{~_ψ}` maps to `[¬ψ̃]_{~_ψ̃}` under `r ↦ r̃`.
