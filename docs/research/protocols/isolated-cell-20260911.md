# Protocol: isolated-cell detection as a second non-linear observation — 2026-09-11

**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), ninth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** pending (Codex, gate 1, requested on the gathering PR before implementation).
**Why this unit:** the [eighth unit](../2026-09-11-block-majority.md) found that under block majority every closed rule closes by one of two mechanisms, exact commutation with the observation or collapse of the observed successor to a constant field. Its checkpoint records that nothing may be inferred about whether that shape is general. This unit takes the simplest other non-linear observation, rule 4 applied once (a cell reads 1 exactly when it is an isolated one, window `010`), and freezes the commute-or-collapse shape as a falsifiable prediction. Rule 4 is also the first observation that is not self-dual, so complement conjugation carries the observation itself to another one; the unit records that as a covariance of the contract rather than an invariance of a table.

## 1. The transformation and its costs

`π(S) = F_4(S)` on the `n`-ring, `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`: `π(S)_i = 1` iff `S_{i−1} S_i S_{i+1} = 010`. Definitional facts, verified from the rule table and by observation-only enumeration before freezing and not counted as results: rule 4 is reflection-symmetric and not self-dual, its complement conjugate being rule 223 (`F_223(S) = ¬F_4(¬S)`); its image never contains two adjacent ones; it is non-surjective, with image fractions 0.281, 0.227, 0.184, 0.148, 0.120, 0.097, 0.079 at rings 6 to 12; its fibers range from singletons to 29, 51, 90, 158, 277, 486, 853 states at those rings, the largest fiber at every ring being the preimage of the all-zeros field, which contains every state with no isolated one, in particular `0ⁿ`, `1ⁿ`, and every state whose ones come in blocks of two or more. Costs: forward radius 1; no inverse; information lost varies by fiber, from zero bits on singleton fibers to `log₂ 853` bits at ring 12; the image is a proper subset, so a factor `B` is defined on the image only.

Closure of `F_r` under `π`, exact commutation `K(n)`, the factor `B`, and the refinement depth `h_*` are defined exactly as in the eighth unit's protocol, Section 1, with 232 replaced by 4. A rule is a **collapse rule** at ring `n` when it is closed and its factor is constant on the image, that is, `π(F_r S)` is the same field for every `S`. Let `Z(n)` be the set of collapse rules.

## 2. Frozen predictions

- **N1 (commuters close; theorem control).** For every `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`, every rule in `K(n)` is closed. `K(n)` contains at least `{0, 4, 170, 204, 240, 232}`: rule 0 commutes with rule 4 because rule 4 fixes `0ⁿ`; the identity and the shifts commute with every elementary rule; rule 4 commutes with itself; rule 232 commutes with rule 4 on rings 6 to 12 by the eighth unit's exhaustive commuter set. Rule 255 is **not** in `K(n)`: rule 4 does not fix `1ⁿ`, so `F_4 F_255 = 0ⁿ ≠ 1ⁿ = F_255 F_4`. This is the eighth unit's binding correction applied: a constant commutes with an observation only when the observation fixes that uniform configuration.
- **N2 (a collapse rule that does not commute; control).** `255 ∈ Z(n) \ K(n)` at every ring, because `F_255 S = 1ⁿ` and `π(1ⁿ) = 0ⁿ`, so `π ∘ F_255` is the constant `0ⁿ`. Hence `C(n) ≠ K(n)` is frozen, not reported.
- **N3 (commute-or-collapse; the unit's question).** `C(n) = K(n) ∪ Z(n)` at every `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`: every closed rule either commutes exactly with rule 4 or has a constant factor on the image. A closed rule whose factor is neither `F_r` restricted to the image nor a constant is a failure of N3; every such rule is listed with its factor table, and the failure, if it occurs, is the unit's main finding. Whether `C(n)` and `K(n)` are the same sets at every ring is reported.
- **N4 (the remaining affine rules do not close).** The eight affine rules `{60, 90, 102, 105, 150, 153, 165, 195}` are not closed at any `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`, scored by fiber-successor inequality. Frozen witness: among the four states `0ⁿ`, one block `11` in zeros, one block `111` in zeros, and one block `110111` in zeros (at `n = 6` that is the whole ring), all of which lie in the all-zeros fiber at every `n ≥ 6`, the pair `(0ⁿ, W_r)` has different observed successors, with `W_r` fixed per rule: `11` for 60, 102, 150, 153, 195; `111` for 105; `110111` for 90 and 165. Verified by direct enumeration at every ring 6 to 12 before this re-freeze (the earlier hand check for rule 90 with `11` and `111` was wrong, see Section 5); the `11` block does not separate 90, 105 or 165, the `111` block does not separate 90, 153 or 195, and `110111` separates only 90 and 165. The verifier scores non-closure directly and reports the witness clause separately, so a wrong witness cannot mask a real non-closure; a rule for which the named pair does not separate is a failure of the witness clause even if the rule is not closed by another pair, and both facts are reported.
- **N5 (history census).** `h_*` for all 256 rules at every `n ∈ {6, …, 12}`; `h_* = 0` exactly on `C(n)`. Magnitudes, the maximum per ring, the rules attaining it, the count of rules whose depth changes with `n`, and the comparison with the eighth unit's maxima 2, 4, 3, 4, 7, 8, 16 are reported, not predicted.
- **N6 (symmetries: reflection invariance, complement covariance).** Reflection: `C(n)`, `K(n)`, `Z(n)` and the depth table are invariant under `r ↦ mirror(r)`, because rule 4 is reflection-symmetric. Complement: the tables are **not** predicted invariant. The verifier computes the same census for the observation rule 223 and the frozen prediction is covariance: `r ∈ C_4(n)` iff `r̃ ∈ C_223(n)`, the same for `K` and `Z`, and `h_*` agrees under `r ↦ r̃`, where `r̃` is the complement conjugate. Any table that is complement-invariant outright (`C_4(n) = C_223(n)` as sets) is reported as such and not claimed in advance.
- **N7 (relation to the earlier criteria).** Frozen: `CLOSED_32 ⊄ C_4(n)` at every ring, since rule 90 lies in `CLOSED_32` and is not closed by N4. Reported: whether `C_4(n) ⊆ C_232(n)` or the reverse, the intersection of the two closed sets and of the two commuter sets, and whether `C_4(n) ⊆ CLOSED_32`.
