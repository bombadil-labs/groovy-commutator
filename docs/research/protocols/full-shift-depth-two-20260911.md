# Protocol: full-shift depth at most two by sparse reachability, and the depth gap — 2026-09-11

**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), fifteenth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** pending Codex (OpenAI) gate 1 on the gathering PR.
**Why this unit:** the [fourteenth unit](../2026-09-11-depth-one-certificate.md) found that, for depth, the periodic configurations do not exhaust the full shift: under 232, 200, 22 and 102, 54 rules have refinement depth at most one at every ring but not on the full shift, each with a bi-infinitely extendable violating walk that lies on no closed walk. Two questions follow directly. First, how deep are those rules on the full shift: is their full-shift depth exactly two, or more? Second, does the same gap recur one level up, at depth two? A ring certificate at depth two would need boolean powers of 4096 × 4096 matrices per rule, out of budget; but the **full-shift** question at depth two needs only reachability in a sparse 4096-vertex graph, which is cheap for every rule and observation. So this unit certifies full-shift depth at most two for all eight observations and all 256 rules, compares it with exhaustive depth two on rings 3 to 14, and turns the fourteenth unit's abstract gap witnesses into explicit pairs of eventually periodic configurations.

## 1. Objects and definitions

Observations `ψ ∈ {232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90, 150}`, as in units thirteen and fourteen. `h_* ≤ 2` at ring `n` means `P_3 = P_2`: three steps of observed history determine the fourth. `D²_ψ(n) = {r : h_*(r, ψ, n) ≤ 2}`, computed exhaustively for `3 ≤ n ≤ 14` (four applications of the rule per state).

**The depth-two pair graph `G²_{ψ,r}`.** Vertices are the 4096 six-cell pair blocks. There is an edge from block `(x_0..5, y_0..5)` to `(x_1..6, y_1..6)` exactly when the seven-cell pair block `(x_0..6, y_0..6)` satisfies the three agreements at its centre: `ψ(x)_3 = ψ(y)_3` (radius 1), `ψ(F_r x)_3 = ψ(F_r y)_3` (radius 2) and `ψ(F_r² x)_3 = ψ(F_r² y)_3` (radius 3). Ring-`n` pair configurations with `ψ`, `ψ ∘ F_r` and `ψ ∘ F_r²` agreeing are exactly the closed walks of length `n` in `G²_{ψ,r}`. A **violating walk** is a three-edge walk `v_0 → v_1 → v_2 → v_3` reading a nine-cell pair block `(x_{−4..4}, y_{−4..4})` whose three seven-cell sub-blocks are edges and whose fourth observed successors disagree at the centre, `ψ(F_r³ x)_0 ≠ ψ(F_r³ y)_0` (radius 4). `V²_{ψ,r}` is the set of `(v_0, v_3)` over violating walks, from the `4^9 = 262,144` nine-cell pair blocks.

**Full-shift depth two.** `r` has `h_* ≤ 2` on the full shift iff no violating walk is bi-infinitely extendable in `G²_{ψ,r}`: `v_0` reachable from a vertex on a cycle and `v_3` reaching a vertex on a cycle (same argument as the thirteenth unit's Section 1, with the radii 1, 2, 3 for the agreements and 4 for the violation). `FS²_ψ` is the set of such rules; `FS¹_ψ` is the fourteenth unit's full-shift depth-one set, read from its result file. Reachability and cycles are computed by graph search on the sparse edge list (at most four out-edges per vertex); no matrix power is taken and no ring certificate at depth two is claimed.

**Theorem facts (proved as in units thirteen and fourteen).** Full-shift depth at most two implies depth at most two at every ring (periodic configurations are configurations). `FS¹_ψ ⊆ FS²_ψ` and `D¹_ψ(n) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` (refinement is monotone in depth). Divisibility: `D²_ψ(kn) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)`.

**Full-shift depth two under neighbor parity (deduction made in this unit, for M4).** The sixth unit's record scoped its theorem to every finite ring and did not claim the infinite line; the promotion to the full shift is made here, not attributed to it. On connected `ℤ`, `π(x) = π(y)` iff `y = x` or `y = ¬x` (a parity field determines a configuration up to one global complement), so every `π`-agreeing pair is a complement pair, exactly as on a ring. A complement pair `(x, ¬x)` stays complementary for one step iff the complement response `g_r(x) ≡ 1` at every cell, and for two steps iff `x ∈ L = W_1 ∩ F^{−1}(W_1)`; these are cellwise conditions, so every eight-cell window of a configuration in `L` is an `L`-admissible 8-word in the sixth unit's sense. The sixth unit certified that every `L`-admissible 8-word has equal adjacent values of `g_r ∘ F²`; hence for `x ∈ L` the field `g_r(F²x)` is constant along connected `ℤ`. If that constant is 1, `F³(¬x) = ¬F³x` and the pair stays complementary, so `π(F³x) = π(F³(¬x))`; if it is 0, `F³(¬x) = F³x` and the pair becomes equal, absorbing. In both cases a pair agreeing on `π`, `π∘F`, `π∘F²` agrees on `π∘F³`, which is `h_* ≤ 2` on the full shift for all 256 rules. The verifier's `FS²_102` computation is an independent check of this deduction, not its source.

**Explicit witness pairs for the fourteenth unit's gap.** For each of the 54 pairs `(ψ, r)` in the fourteenth unit's `L7` failure record, that record names an extendable violating walk `(v_0, v_3)` in the depth-one graph `G¹_{ψ,r}`. The verifier reconstructs, by breadth-first search on `G¹_{ψ,r}`: a cycle `C_L` and a path from a vertex of `C_L` to `v_0`; the violating walk itself; and a path from `v_3` to a vertex of a cycle `C_R`. Concatenating the cell contents gives a pair `(x, y)` of bi-infinite configurations, periodic to the left with the period of `C_L` and to the right with the period of `C_R`, described by the finite data `(left cycle word, left path word, violating block, right path word, right cycle word)`. The graph path itself is the certificate: by the fourteenth unit's pair-graph semantics, a walk through `G¹_{ψ,r}` is exactly a pair agreeing on `ψ` and `ψ∘F_r` at every cell it covers, and the two cycles make the agreement hold on the whole bi-infinite pair. The verifier additionally replays the pair on a finite window covering three periods on each side and checks `ψ(x) = ψ(y)` and `ψ(F_r x) = ψ(F_r y)` on the trimmed interior and `ψ(F_r² x)_0 ≠ ψ(F_r² y)_0` at the centre; the deepest check, `ψ∘F_r²`, has radius 3, so the interior trims three cells at each window boundary (the trimmed margin is 3). This replay is an implementation control on the reconstruction, not the certificate.

## 2. Frozen predictions

- **M1 (full shift implies every ring; theorem control).** For all eight observations and every `3 ≤ n ≤ 14`, `FS²_ψ ⊆ D²_ψ(n)`.
- **M2 (monotonicity and divisibility; theorem controls).** `FS¹_ψ ⊆ FS²_ψ` for all eight; `D¹_ψ(n) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` for every exhaustive ring, with `D¹_ψ(n)` read from the fourteenth unit's result; `D²_ψ(kn) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` for `3 ≤ n < kn ≤ 14`.
- **M3 (the fourteenth unit's gap rules have full-shift depth exactly two; falsifiable bet).** All 54 pairs `(ψ, r)` in the fourteenth unit's L7 failure record lie in `FS²_ψ`. A failure lists the pairs whose full-shift depth is at least three, each with an extendable violating walk in `G²_{ψ,r}`.
- **M4 (parity is depth at most two on the full shift; theorem control by the deduction in Section 1).** `FS²_102` is all 256 rules. The control rests on the sixth unit's finite certificate (no violating `L`-admissible 8-word at all) together with this unit's deduction promoting it to connected `ℤ`; the sixth unit itself claimed finite rings only. A failure would refute the deduction and is recorded as such.
- **M5 (the depth-two gap; two named sets, one bet).** Two sets are reported for each observation and must not be conflated: the **ring-14 gap** `Γ²_{ψ,14} = D²_ψ(14) \ FS²_ψ`, and the **all-tested-ring gap** `Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]} = (⋂_{3 ≤ n ≤ 14} D²_ψ(n)) \ FS²_ψ`, the bounded analogue of the fourteenth unit's "depth at most one at every ring but not on the full shift". Neither is an all-ring statement, since no depth-two ring certificate exists. **Bet:** `Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]}` is nonempty for exactly `ψ ∈ {232, 200, 22}` among the eight; the failure record names every observation on the wrong side. `Γ²_{ψ,14}` is reported without a bet. Each gap rule in either set is listed with one extendable violating walk in `G²_{ψ,r}`, the endpoints' cycle and reachability facts and strong components.
- **M6 (explicit witness pairs; theorem control on the verifier).** For every one of the 54 fourteenth-unit gap pairs the reconstructed eventually periodic pair `(x, y)` passes the finite-window check of Section 1. The finite data of every witness is recorded.
- **M7 (symmetries; theorem controls).** Complement covariance `FS²_{ψ̃} = {r̃ : r ∈ FS²_ψ}` and `D²_{ψ̃}(n) = {r̃ : r ∈ D²_ψ(n)}`, and reflection covariance, for all eight observations at every exhaustive ring.
