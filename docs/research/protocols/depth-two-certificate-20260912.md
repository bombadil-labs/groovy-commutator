# Protocol: refinement depth at most two, certified at every ring by sparse boolean powers — 2026-09-12

**Status:** frozen before implementation and evaluation. Nothing run.
**Program:** [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md), eighteenth unit.
**Authored by:** Claude Code, Fable 5.1. **Protocol review:** pending gate 1 by an independent collaborating agent (Codex / OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol requested) on the gathering PR.
**Why this unit:** the [seventeenth unit's checkpoint](../checkpoints/representation-invariants.md) left three candidates: full-shift depth four for the deep rules, a structural account of the deep rules, and "a depth-two ring certificate if a cheaper matrix method is found". Depth four is not planned here: it needs a cost decision from Myk under the 2026-09-12 CI-cost boundary and is a separate discussion. Between the other two, the certificate is the one that can be frozen today as a falsifiable protocol: its objects already exist (the [fifteenth unit's](../2026-09-11-full-shift-depth-two.md) 4096-vertex pair graph `G²_{ψ,r}` and the [fourteenth unit's](../2026-09-11-depth-one-certificate.md) ring criterion, certificate `(k, p)`, and censoring contract), and the cheaper method is concrete: the pair graph has out-degree at most four (a six-cell pair block extends to the right by one of four pair cells), so a boolean power `A^{m} = A^{m−1} · A` is, row by row, the OR of at most four rows of `A^{m−1}`; with rows packed as 64 words of 64 bits this is about one million word operations per power instead of the `4096³` of a dense product, and a scratch benchmark on a random out-degree-4 graph of this size measured about 7.5 ms per power including the hash of the result. The fifteenth unit said such a certificate "would need boolean powers of 4096 × 4096 matrices per rule, out of budget"; the budget objection was to dense powers, and sparsity removes it. A structural account of the deep rules, by contrast, has no frozen object yet: nobody has a candidate invariant to bet on, so a protocol for it would be a plan to look, not a prediction that can fail. It stays a candidate. The scientific question this unit answers is the depth-two analogue of the fourteenth unit's L7: the fifteenth unit found rules with depth at most two at every ring from 3 to 14 but not on the full shift (20, 4, 4, 12, 30, 0, 6, 12 rules under 232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90, 150), and could not say whether they keep depth two at every ring, because no ring certificate existed at depth two and its ring sets had not visibly settled by ring 14 (`|D²_232(n)|` = 198, 200, 198 at rings 12, 13, 14; `|D²_4(n)|` = 105, 102, 101; under 90 on even rings 250, 244, 248, 242, 248 and under 150 on multiples of 3 244, 242, 234). This unit decides `D²_ψ(n)` for every ring `n ≥ 4`, the eventual period of the set sequence, and the all-ring depth-two gap.

## 1. Objects and definitions

Observations `ψ ∈ {232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90, 150}`, as in units thirteen to fifteen; rules `r ∈ 0..255`. `h_* ≤ 2` at ring `n` means `P_3 = P_2` (three steps of observed history determine the fourth); `D²_ψ(n) = {r : h_*(r, ψ, n) ≤ 2}`. For `3 ≤ n ≤ 14` these sets are **read from the fifteenth unit's result file** `results/full_shift_depth_two_20260911.json` with its hash; they are not recomputed. `FS²_ψ` and `FS¹_ψ` are read from the same file (it carries both). `D²_ψ^∞ = ⋂_{n ≥ 4} D²_ψ(n)`, the fourteenth unit's convention (ring 3 is exhaustive-only and reported alongside).

**The depth-two pair graph `G²_{ψ,r}`** is the fifteenth unit's, verbatim: vertices the 4096 six-cell pair blocks; an edge from `(x_0..5, y_0..5)` to `(x_1..6, y_1..6)` exactly when the seven-cell pair block agrees at its centre on `ψ` (radius 1), `ψ∘F_r` (radius 2) and `ψ∘F_r²` (radius 3). `A_{ψ,r}` is its boolean adjacency matrix; every vertex has at most four out-edges. A **violating walk** is a three-edge walk `v_0 → v_1 → v_2 → v_3` reading a nine-cell pair block whose three seven-cell sub-blocks are edges and whose fourth observed successors disagree at the centre, `ψ(F_r³ x)_0 ≠ ψ(F_r³ y)_0` (radius 4); `V²_{ψ,r}` is the set of `(v_0, v_3)` over violating walks, from the `4^9 = 262,144` nine-cell pair blocks. All of this is the fifteenth unit's code path (`depth_two_graph`), reused.

**Ring criterion (theorem; the thirteenth and fourteenth units' proof with the graph replaced).** Ring-`n` pair configurations agreeing on `ψ`, `ψ∘F_r`, `ψ∘F_r²` are exactly the closed walks of length `n` in `G²_{ψ,r}` (a closed walk of length `n` unrolls to an `n`-periodic pair, and every local function evaluated on the periodic unrolling equals its ring evaluation). Such a pair violates depth two at some cell iff the closed walk contains a violating three-edge walk, iff, for `n ≥ 4`, some `(v_0, v_3) ∈ V²_{ψ,r}` has `(A_{ψ,r}^{n−3})[v_3, v_0] = 1`. So for `n ≥ 4`: `r ∉ D²_ψ(n)` iff `∃ (v_0, v_3) ∈ V²_{ψ,r}: (A^{n−3})[v_3, v_0] = 1`.

**Closed-walk pruning (lemma).** A return walk `v_3 ⇝ v_0` together with the violating walk `v_0 → v_3` is a closed walk, so `v_0` and `v_3` lie in one strong component. A violating walk whose ends lie in different strong components contributes to no ring; if `V²_{ψ,r}` has no violating walk with both ends in a common strong component (the **closed-violation set** `V²_{cl}` is empty), then `r ∈ D²_ψ(n)` for every `n ≥ 4` with no power computed, recorded as `trivially certified` with `(k, p)` undefined and no censoring possible. Consequence (theorem): every `r ∈ FS¹_ψ` has `V²_{cl} = ∅`, since a closed violating walk unrolls to a periodic pair agreeing on `ψ` and `ψ∘F_r` everywhere, whose full-shift depth one forces agreement on `ψ∘F_r²` everywhere, and then, applied to the pair `(F_r x, F_r y)`, on `ψ∘F_r³`, contradicting the violation. This is checked, not assumed (P1).

**Sparse packed powers.** For each `(ψ, r)` with `V²_{cl} ≠ ∅`, `A^m` is held as 4096 rows of 64 unsigned 64-bit words; `A^0 = I`, and `A^m[u, ·] = OR_{w ∈ out(u)} A^{m−1}[w, ·]`, four gathers and three ORs per power. This is exactly boolean matrix multiplication; no approximation. After each power the bit `b_m = [∃ (v_0, v_3) ∈ V²_{cl}: A^m[v_3, v_0] = 1]` is recorded, so that `r ∈ D²_ψ(n)` iff `b_{n−3} = 0`. **Exact repeat detection:** the SHA-256 of each power's bytes is stored in a table indexed by exponent, and the power itself is not stored. At the first exponent `k + p` whose hash already appears at exponent `k`, `A^k` is recomputed from `A^0` by `k` further powers and compared to `A^{k+p}` byte for byte; if equal, the certificate is `(k, p)` (least `k`, then least `p`, by construction of the scan); if not (a hash collision), the collision is recorded and the scan continues, so a collision can only delay a certificate, never falsify one. Then `b` is periodic from `k` with period `p`, and membership of `r` in `D²_ψ(n)` is decided for every `n ≥ 4` by the finite list `b_1, …, b_{k+p−1}`.

**Power cap and censoring contract (frozen, the fourteenth unit's, unchanged).** The scan stops at the first confirmed repeat or at the cap `A^{1024}`, whichever comes first; the cap is a constant of the verifier and is recorded in the canonical artifact's parameters. A pair whose powers have not repeated by the cap is **censored**: no `(k, p)` is claimed, its membership in `D²_ψ(n)` is decided only for `4 ≤ n ≤ 1027` and marked open beyond, and any observation-level all-ring verdict (P3, P4) that depends on a censored rule is itself recorded as censored, not inferred. No larger cap or rescue computation is introduced after the run.

**Certificate at the observation level.** Per observation, `D²_ψ(n)` is eventually periodic in `n` with period dividing the least common multiple `L_ψ` of the rules' periods, from `N⁰_ψ = 3 + max_r k_r`; the verifier reports the least eventual period `P_ψ` and the least onset ring `N_ψ ≥ 4` from which `D²_ψ(n + P_ψ) = D²_ψ(n)` for all `n ≥ N_ψ`, the certified list `D²_ψ(4), …, D²_ψ(N_ψ + P_ψ − 1)`, and `D²_ψ^∞` (an intersection over one full period from the onset, hence over all `n ≥ 4`). Divisibility (theorem, fifteenth unit): `D²_ψ(kn) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)`.

**All-ring depth-two gap.** `Γ²_{ψ,∞} = D²_ψ^∞ \ FS²_ψ`, the certified analogue of the fifteenth unit's rings-3-to-14 gap `Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]}`; `Γ²_{ψ,∞} ⊆ Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]}` always (more rings intersected). `Γ²_{102,∞} = ∅` by theorem (`FS²_102` is all 256 rules, fifteenth unit M4).

**Kernel facts (theorem, from the seventh and fifteenth units).** Depth under a linear observation depends only on its kernel. Rule 150 is bijective on rings with `3 ∤ n`, so `D²_150(n)` is all 256 rules there. On odd `n`, `ker 90 = ker 102`, so `D²_90(n) = D²_102(n) = 256` for odd `n` (the fifteenth unit's M4 full-shift deduction gives `D²_102(n) = 256` for every `n`).

## 2. Frozen predictions

- **P1 (criterion exact; theorem and consistency controls).** For all eight observations and every `4 ≤ n ≤ 14`, the set decided by the criterion equals the fifteenth unit's recorded `D²_ψ(n)`. For every `(ψ, r)` with `r ∈ FS¹_ψ`, `V²_{cl} = ∅` (theorem). `FS²_ψ ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` for every certified `n`. Divisibility `D²_ψ(kn) ⊆ D²_ψ(n)` for every certified pair `4 ≤ n < kn ≤ N_ψ + P_ψ − 1` (censored observations: over the decided rings). Implementation control: for a frozen sample of pairs — for each observation, the three smallest rule numbers with `V²_{cl} ≠ ∅` — the packed sparse powers `A^1, …, A^{40}` equal the fourteenth unit's dense float-matmul boolean powers byte for byte.
- **P2 (certificates; reported, with censoring).** For every `(ψ, r)` one of: `trivially certified` (`V²_{cl} = ∅`), `(k, p)`, or `censored`; per observation the largest `k`, `L_ψ`, `P_ψ`, `N_ψ`, the certified list and `D²_ψ^∞`; the count of pairs in each status and the total number of powers taken; any hash collision.
- **P3 (eventual periods; bets and theorem controls).** (a) Bet: for `ψ ∈ {232, 4, 32, 200}`, `P_ψ = 1` (the sequence becomes constant); `N_ψ` reported without a bet (the recorded sizes have not settled by ring 14, so the onset is genuinely open; a failure of (a) reports the actual period and the rules that oscillate). (b) Bet: `P_22 = 3` (the depth-one certificate found period 3 with onset 28, and the recorded depth-two sizes at rings 9 to 14 depend on `n mod 3`). (c) Theorem control: `D²_102(n) = 256` for every certified `n`, `D²_90(n) = 256` for every certified odd `n`, `D²_150(n) = 256` for every certified `n` with `3 ∤ n`. (d) Bets on the linear observations, mirroring the fourteenth unit's depth-one periods (4 and 6): `P_90` is a multiple of 4 and `P_150` a multiple of 6. A failure reports the actual period. The recorded sizes on even rings under 90 (250, 244, 248, 242, 248) and on multiples of 3 under 150 (244, 242, 234) leave both open.
- **P4 (the all-ring depth-two gap; the unit's main bet).** For each of the eight observations, `Γ²_{ψ,∞} = Γ²_{ψ,[3,14]}`: every rule with depth at most two on every ring 3 to 14 but not on the full shift keeps depth at most two at every ring. Hence the all-ring gap is nonempty for exactly the seven observations other than 102 (20, 4, 4, 12, 30, 6, 12 rules), certifying at depth two what the fourteenth unit's L7 failure showed at depth one: periodic configurations do not exhaust the full shift for depth, at every ring, not only up to 14. A failure lists each observation with a rule leaving `D²_ψ(n)` at some `n > 14`, with the least such `n` and the closed violating walk `(v_0, v_3)` with `b_{n−3} = 1` that realizes it; a censored observation is reported as censored, not as a failure.
- **P5 (symmetries; theorem controls).** Complement covariance `D²_{ψ̃}(n) = {r̃ : r ∈ D²_ψ(n)}` and reflection covariance `D²_{mirror ψ}(n) = {mirror(r) : r ∈ D²_ψ(n)}` at every certified ring, the conjugate and mirrored observations' sets computed by the criterion; and equality of the certificate status and `(k, p)` between `(ψ, r)` and `(ψ̃, r̃)` and between `(ψ, r)` and `(mirror ψ, mirror r)`, since conjugation and reflection are relabelings of the pair graph (reflection reverses walks, so `A` transposes, which preserves `(k, p)`).

## 3. Artifacts and cost

Output `results/depth_two_certificate_20260912.json` with source hashes, parameters (cap 1024, sample for the dense cross-check), per-pair status and `(k, p)` and `|V²|`, `|V²_{cl}|`, per-observation `P_ψ`, `N_ψ`, certified lists, `D²_ψ^∞`, `Γ²_{ψ,∞}`, and the P1–P5 verdicts with every violation. Registered in `scripts/check_result_integrity.py`. Note under `docs/research/2026-09-12-depth-two-certificate.md`; the Program's non-injective table gains a row.

**Cost and the CI-cost boundary.** Pair graphs: 8 observations plus their complement conjugates and the mirror images of the asymmetric ones, at most about 4,100 graphs, each from the 16,384 seven-cell and 262,144 nine-cell pair blocks evaluated vectorially — the fifteenth unit measured about ten minutes for this part alone, including exhaustive rings it does not repeat here. Powers: only pairs with `V²_{cl} ≠ ∅`, at most the rules outside `FS¹_ψ` (154, 175, 175, 120, 163, 16, 126, 158 for the eight observations, about 1,100, roughly doubled by conjugates and mirrors), at about 7.5 ms per power; if typical certificates repeat within a hundred powers this is 15 to 30 minutes, and the cap bounds the worst case at about 8 s per pair, roughly five hours if every pair ran to the cap. Memory is one current power (2 MB), the adjacency, and a table of hashes; a repeat confirmation recomputes rather than stores. **So the canonical run is expected at 30 to 60 minutes and bounded above by about six hours: it does not fit the ~10-minute automatic-CI budget of Myk's 2026-09-12 boundary and will be run once outside GitHub Actions from the pinned, reviewed implementation, with execution provenance recorded in the note and the result file.** Automatic CI on this unit's files runs only the seconds-scale `scripts/check_result_integrity.py` tier and the verifier's `--self-test` (the P1 dense cross-check on the frozen sample plus one rule certified and compared against the fifteenth unit's ring-14 set; under two minutes, writes nothing). The full byte-for-byte replay is a `workflow_dispatch`-only workflow requiring an authorization input, on the pattern of `research-full-shift-depth-three-linear.yml`, created that way from the start and never triggered automatically; the independent replay required for gate 2 is performed off-Actions by author or reviewer.

## 4. Not claimed

- Nothing about depth three or beyond at any ring beyond what the sixteenth and seventeenth units already state; a rule outside `D²_ψ^∞` has `h_* ≥ 3` on some ring and nothing more is certified about it. No ring certificate at depth three is given or implied by this method's success at depth two (depth three would need 65,536-vertex powers, 128 MB per packed power).
- Nothing about full-shift depth: `FS²_ψ` is the fifteenth unit's and is not recomputed; this unit certifies the ring side of the gap only.
- Nothing about observations outside the eight, or about rings 1 and 2; ring 3 is reported from the fifteenth unit's exhaustive set and is not covered by the criterion.
- A censored pair is a reported limit, not a finding; the 1024 cap is a verifier constant, not a claim about the index of any graph.
- No structural account of the gap rules: the certificate says which rules have all-ring depth two and full-shift depth at least three, not why.
- No Class IV, universality, novelty, renormalization, dimension, physical or metaphysical claim.
