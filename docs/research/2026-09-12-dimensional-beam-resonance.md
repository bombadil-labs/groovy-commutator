## Why this note

The Dimensional Closure Program already defines the Turtle Beam as more than recursive representability: the target is recurring dimensional construction, coherent lower-dimensional trajectories, new native higher-dimensional possibilities, and organization increasingly sustained by the unfolding itself.

A 2026-09-12 discussion sharpens that target in two ways.

First, recursive self-similarity is not enough. A lifted system may preserve ancestry while the correspondence remains visible only to an external verifier. The stronger target is that structural self-correspondence eventually becomes **causally consequential inside the dynamics**.

The user's term **resonance**, motivated by a sat/chit/ananda analogy, names the somatic/intuitive model behind this refinement: ananda as the felt event of being recognizing itself through self-similarity. This language is motivational only. It is not scientific evidence, a consciousness claim, a Buddhist-doctrinal claim, or a metaphysical conclusion.

Operationally, a future beam experiment should compare otherwise matched encounters in which one partner is structurally homologous/self-similar to the focal organization and the other is a matched non-homologous control. The question is whether the matching relation itself changes what happens next: preservation, coupling, synchronization, repair, amplification, or access to new reachable states are examples of possible response variables, but any concrete test must freeze one before evaluation.

A transported recognition relation would therefore be stronger than a commuting square checked from outside. The beam target becomes: **not only the represented trajectory lifts; some endogenous response to self-correspondence must also survive the lift.**

## A separate scaling conjecture: history depth may track dimensional level

The accepted full-field interface-history result supplies one suggestive datum. In the frozen touching-strip 2D family, complete retained fields remain globally conflicting through one retained lag and first become globally deterministic for a small set of representations at two retained lags. Full P15 is one of those passing cells on both tested rings, while every tested local radius through two still conflicts.

This raises a clean but untested conjecture:

> In a recursively comparable dimensional tower, the minimal retained history depth needed for predictive closure may grow with dimensional level, perhaps schematically as `h_min(d)=d`.

This is **not** established by the current result. Important cautions:

- `h=2` means current state plus two previous retained states, not two time slices total;
- the existing datum comes from one finite 2D touching-strip representation, not from a matched 1D/2D/3D tower;
- finite-ring aliasing, observation choice, cadence, locality radius, and represented state family can all change minimal sufficient history;
- history depth is not spatial dimension, and no completed result should be reinterpreted as proving otherwise.

A serious test would require a family of adjacent dimensional rungs with the same lift/interpreter semantics and a predeclared definition of minimal sufficient history at each rung. Only then would it make sense to test whether the first sufficient depth follows `0,1,2,3,...`, grows by some other law, or has no stable relation to dimensional level.

## Minimal spacetime support and the Assembly Theory analogy

The history/locality split suggests a stronger object than history depth alone. A representation may have a characteristic **minimal spacetime support**: how much retained history, spatial extent, and coordinate information are jointly required before the next represented state becomes single-valued.

Schematic bookkeeping might therefore look like

`Sigma_d = (history depth, spatial extent, retained channels, support cost, ...)`.

The user's Assembly Theory comparison is useful motivation here: instead of asking only whether a state exists or is predictable, ask what minimum structured support is required to reconstruct or determine it at dimensional level `d`, and whether repeated motifs reduce that cost through reuse.

This is Cronin-adjacent territory, not an established identification with Assembly Theory. Molecular assembly index counts minimal constructive steps under a specific assembly grammar; a predictive spacetime-support index would instead measure the minimum retained causal information sufficient for a declared dynamical prediction. They should remain explicitly different quantities unless a later theorem or frozen experiment relates them.

The immediate research opportunity is therefore to define a representation-relative **predictive assembly support** exactly on an already accepted finite family, before asking whether any such support measure scales through the dimensional tower.

## Dated integration: resources, evidence boundaries, and reusable leverage

This 2026-09-12 addendum preserves the earlier motivation above while incorporating subsequent accepted evidence. It introduces no experiment, result, or change to a frozen protocol. The refinement remains inside [Dimensional Closure](2026-09-09-dimensional-closure-program.md), not a new Program.

### A history threshold is conditional, not a dimension counter

The [full-field audit](2026-09-12-interface-history-global.md) found two-lag sufficiency on rings 6 and 7. The now-accepted [cross-width audit](2026-09-12-interface-history-cross-width.md) finds that masks 11, 13, and 15 remain globally deterministic on width 8 but fail globally on width 9, under the same D2/two-lag observation. Thus two lags are not a width-uniform threshold for that representation. Width-9 full-field conflicts cannot be repaired by increasing spatial radius alone there; they do not imply monotone failure at larger widths.

Any proposed minimum history must name the law, observation, admissible family, cadence, prediction target, and locality contract as well as dimension. Complete-state observation of deterministic Markov dynamics needs no retained past. A selected lossy observation can need memory; that fact alone supplies no universal h_min(d)=d law. History depth counts previous samples, so two lags mean three observed time slices. Prediction of the next observed field is weaker than reconstruction of the full physical state.

A joint resource profile should generally be a Pareto frontier rather than one preferred tuple: history depth, spatial radius, retained coordinates, and construction/intervention cost need not have the same minimizer. A tradeoff requires a declared budget or partial ordering, not an unpriced claim that one resource has become another.

### Predictive support is not constructive assembly

The follow-up [PAS unit #172](https://github.com/bombadil-labs/groovy-commutator/pull/172) concerns a minimum decision-table reduct: choose a fixed subset of lag/site/channel coordinates separating every pair of allowed records with different next fields. It is a family-level predictive sufficiency cost under fixed atoms and target, not the complexity of one individual state, not an entropy, and not a construction history. Its reuse-grammar diagnostic was removed at Gate 1; no result here reinstates it.

In contrast, Sharma et al., [Assembly theory explains and quantifies selection and evolution](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06600-9), define assembly index through shortest recursive construction paths from basic parts with intermediate objects available for reuse. Their ensemble assembly quantity also uses copy number. This motivates asking a future construction question but does not identify PAS with assembly index. A genuine construction-cost experiment would need explicit elementary objects, legal joins, reusable intermediates, output equivalence, and charged preparation resources.

### The free-lunch analogy needs a costed task

Levin's [research overview](https://drmichaellevin.org/research/) describes exploiting physical and computational affordances for adaptive solutions without separately evolving every solution. That is broader than merely storing a previously learned program. The connection to this Program is a proposed question about reusable leverage, not a finding that our beam already supplies it.

For a future cost experiment, compare the same task under homologous inherited organization and matched alternatives while charging preparation, storage, locality, latency, and interventions consistently. Adding free primitives can only weakly lower a minimum by definition; that monotonicity is a null, not an observed benefit. A scientifically informative gain must survive that accounting and matched controls. Neither a small PAS value nor a response difference alone establishes such a gain.
