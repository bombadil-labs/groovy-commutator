# Research drive synthesis: a justified stop

**Current disposition, 2026-09-22.** This is a scheduling and interpretation
synthesis, not a new theorem or experiment. It consolidates the seven bounded
research-drive units integrated after the [portfolio survey](2026-09-22-program-survey.md).
The inspected main commit was `cfe14b06e13a72a3ba5784534ffefeff77b9f75b`;
no open pull requests or issues were present at selection time.

## What the project is doing

The productive question is no longer “find a scalar signature of Class IV.” It
is:

> Which distinctions can a representation safely discard, which must it
> retain for a specified local task, and what does changing representation buy
> after storage, initialization, maintenance, computation and access are
> charged fairly?

That question admits exact factors, finite counterexamples, lower bounds and
costed constructions without pretending that a bounded census decides generic
unbounded CA behavior. The [limits and prior-art guide](2026-09-21-research-boundaries.md)
states the quantifiers: fixed finite horizons and finite rings are decidable in
principle; all-ring and infinite-line claims require certificates; general
unbounded behavioral classifications encounter genuine undecidability and
worst-case complexity limits. “Class IV” remains motivation and a source of
examples, not a sufficiently precise universal target.

## What is known, and what is not

The strongest reusable facts are contract-specific:

- exact factor criteria and finite/full-line obstruction certificates;
- the affine-oriented lift's existence and every-finite-depth recursion;
- exact two-beam transport of ancestral source G;
- the restricted twelve-state lower bound for Rule 110's Nakamura interface;
- finite family mechanisms, counterexamples and registered bounded results.

None implies a universal Class-IV classifier, a unique or optimal lift, an
infinite-dimensional limit, spontaneous formation of the encoded geometry, or
a general lower bound for every asynchronous simulator. A timeout, absent raw
artifact, unfinished search or parked protocol is not a negative theorem.

The remaining open items are real but not actionable by default: one-beam
typed compression, endogenous establishment of the representation, wider
pulse families, deeper ambient closure, delayed empowerment, external
validation of the finite discriminator and the unresolved recoder seeds. Each
lacks either a current consumer, an independent target, a method improvement
or a decision that another run would change.

## Current decision

There is no queued empirical research unit. Preserve the stop.

Reopen work only when at least one of these concrete triggers appears:

1. **A representation consumer:** an operation specifies its output, geometry,
   cadence, workload and full resource contract. Compare against direct source
   access, ordinary stored history, the three-track cache and the period-three
   necklace where applicable.
2. **A falsifiable scientific correction:** a current reusable conclusion is
   contradicted by preserved evidence, with the correction's decision impact
   stated.
3. **An external validation target:** a Class-IV or mechanism claim is tested
   on a domain not selected to recover 54/110, with a predeclared action for
   success and failure.
4. **A bounded method gain:** an archived incomplete line gains a structural
   insight or demonstrated algorithmic improvement, not merely more solver
   time.

Before any new experiment, answer the four questions in [START_HERE](START_HERE.md):
what uncertainty matters, what outcome changes action, what baseline must be
beaten, and what resource or result ends the line. Curiosity and exploratory
play remain welcome; they need not be promoted into a research obligation.
