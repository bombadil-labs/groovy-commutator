# Class-IV Refinement: what a lift makes visible

This page is the **working synthesis** of the fourth research Program, opened
2026-09-17 by Claude/Fable 5.1 from Myk's GPT handoff. It inherits the
Class-IV thread from [Dimensional Closure and the Commutator Lift](2026-09-09-dimensional-closure-program.md),
whose existence question closed the same day with the
[affine-oriented lift theorem](2026-09-17-affine-oriented-lift-theorem.md).

## Thesis and question

The lift theorem embeds every binary CA exactly as an invariant beam one
dimension up and leaves the ambient completion free. So "is the lifted rule
Class IV?" has no answer: the beam inherits the source by conjugacy and the
ambient rule can do anything else. The question that survives is sharper:

> **Which dynamical distinctions become expressible when representation
> freedom is added, which observables transport across the representation
> change, and does a source rule confer a susceptibility to the
> selective-persistence × sustained-spreading regime on its refinements?**

The Program treats "Class IV" as a phenotype under a declared contract, never
as a label to be reproduced. Its working phenotype is the prospectively
validated 54/110 core: selective predictive persistence together with
sustained disturbance spreading (GPT-5.6 Sol, 2026-09-16; a finite
discriminator that deliberately rejects the disputed 41 and 106).

## Relationship to existing Programs

Dimensional Closure keeps the lift itself, the typed Groovy bundle and the
completion-freedom algebra. Invariants Across Representation Contracts keeps
closure and refinement depth under fixed observations of the *same* rule.
This Program is about *families of rules* related by exact restriction maps,
and about the phenotype's behaviour across those families. It is not forced
into either of the older Programs because its object is the fiber, not the
rule.

## Scope and non-claims

The Program does not seek a universal Class-IV definition, a claim about the
full plane from finite strips, novelty over the storage × spreading
literature (Langton, Wuensche, Feldman–McTague–Crutchfield, Borriello–Walker,
Mediano et al., Mirza 2026), or any result from unrestricted extension
families, where the trivial bolt-on lemma makes every question empty.

## 2026-09-17: the first unit answers the distributional question for one family

The [fiber census](2026-09-17-fiber-census.md) finds that the base ECA
predicts the phenotype distribution of its 4096 height-one preimages: at
height two, 47% of fiber(54) both persists and spreads, against 37% of
fiber(22), 25% of fiber(0), 22% of fiber(90) and 7% of fiber(204). HighLife
and Life are typical members of their fibers, and fiber(22) is not bimodal.
What the ordering means is open: the six fixed bits impose activity (births
on, survivals off) that may explain it without any further property of the
base rule; a census over all 64 fibers is the control. Jev Run 3 reproduced
J1/J3/J5 under operational definitions alone and weakened J4.

## 2026-09-17: the base acts as a whole on the spreading axis

The [64-fiber census](2026-09-17-fiber-census-64.md) answers the confound
the first unit raised. Across all 64 fibers the both-positive fraction is
not additive in the six exposed bits: pairwise interactions, mainly between
a birth bit and a survival bit, predict spreading and the conjunction better
out of sample, while persistence is additive. Whether the base is affine
(`G ≡ c`) does not matter. Fiber(54) remains the most enriched; the middle
of the ordering is not resolved at 128 rules per fiber.

## Initial agenda

1. **Fiber census** (frozen 2026-09-17, first unit): sample the height-one
   fibers of ECAs 54, 22, 90, 204 and 0 and measure the phenotype at height
   two under the fixed contract. Bet: fiber(54) is enriched relative to the
   null, identity and additive bases. Rule 110 has an empty fiber in this
   family; that is a finding, not an omission.
2. **Anisotropic families for 110**: define a restriction map with nonempty
   fibers over non-symmetric ECAs and repeat.
3. **Observer-covariant retention**: replace `R*` by the unstandardized gap
   plus within-fiber residual so the statistic transports under known
   coarse-grainings.
4. **Natural completions of lifted 54/110**: fixed completion policies of
   the affine lift, asked whether generic ambient states regenerate the
   phenotype; Classes I–III roots as controls.
5. **Jev Run 3**, then, only after observation maps are pinned, mixed
   anonymous 1D/2D panels.
