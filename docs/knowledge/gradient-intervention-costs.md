# Can persistent loop information be affordably revised?

Status: planned, not executed. The [loop result](../research/2026-09-10-gradient-loop-invariants.md) supplies a conserved observation for eight source rules. Conservation does not specify who can alter it, what interventions are allowed, or their cost. The [next frozen protocol](../research/protocols/gradient-intervention-costs-20260910.md) makes those choices explicit.

Actions are simultaneous flat XOR masks under a hard changed-edge budget, with touched-site count recorded separately. Enumerate every state and action on the fixed small tori; count distinguishable full-field and loop-vector endpoints at horizons 0,1,4. Compare inherited replicated edits with native target edits, including those outside the lower-dimensional image. Predict and audit minimum loop-changing support and the cost multiplier of replication.

These are external, one-shot, known-state interventions. Integer reachable-outcome counts have a channel-capacity interpretation only under that specific deterministic action/observation contract. They are not evidence of endogenous agency, an implemented local actuator, or an unspecified entropy of possibilities. Mechanism, uncertainty, and accessible control remain distinct.
