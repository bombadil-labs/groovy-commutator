# A rule-field lift still needs a declared interpreter

The [relabeling example](../research/2026-09-10-rule-field-relabeling.md) gives an exact law/state/interpreter transformation. It does not specify a dimensional rule-field lift.

A future proposal must declare its alphabet, per-site state and table storage, decoder, update/transport schedule, admissible family and the earlier constructor it claims to recover. Constancy of transverse rule data is not yet an if-and-only-if criterion. No connection, curvature or selection-mechanism claim is established.
