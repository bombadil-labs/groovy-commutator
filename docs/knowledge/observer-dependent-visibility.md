# Hidden differences have observer- and context-dependent fates

Closure and memory belong to a law, observation, cadence and family together. [Research024](../research/2026-09-08-observer-search.md) exhausts the seven output-complement classes of nonconstant two-cell Boolean observations for every ECA on rings n=8,10,12,14,16. At n=12, 92 rules admit some memoryless observer, while no single tested observer covers that union. Output complementation relabels the entire observed history and preserves closure and memory depth.

The Rule106, n=20, block-parity witness has a hidden adjacent defect translating through macrostep50 and first becoming visible at51. [Research025](../research/2026-09-08-fiber-visibility.md) compares the same initial defect shape in another common context where a finite joint orbit keeps it invisible forever. Fate depends on the shared trajectory as well as defect shape.

These are bounded exact witnesses. Their worst-case visibility time does not estimate typical memory, prove an infinite-lattice scaling law, or establish how an online learner should grow or forget history.
