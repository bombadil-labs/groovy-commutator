# Longer defect trajectories reveal an alternative encoding

The frozen experiment follows 6,912 trajectories through 32 fine ticks:
64 masks, periodic/isolated application, 22 aligned short-period backgrounds,
and two independent cohorts of 16 random backgrounds. All 117,504 sampled
response fields, metrics, and recurrence decisions agree with an independent
packed Boolean implementation.

No initially invalid case returns to the original code. No nonempty response
passes the declared three-shape recurrence screen at periods 2/4/6/8; hence
there are no eligible certified objects for the conditional perturbation stage.
General backgrounds are sampled, not exhaustively enumerated at this horizon.

Two exceptional masks on the zero logical background motivated a separately
committed post-census hypothesis. Its exact check proves a two-row Rule-90
strip with finite matched actions. This follow-up identifies an alternative
invariant family, not a claim that the original recurrence screen succeeded.
