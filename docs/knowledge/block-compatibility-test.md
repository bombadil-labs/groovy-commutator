all rectangles of area at most six, cadences one through three, and fixed
horizontal/vertical displacements bounded by cadence. Equivalent vertical
phases are identified. The upper ternary interpreter stays fixed.

identifications, and 22 literal elementary targets in 11 rule symmetry orbits.

and 110 have no witness within these bounds.

inputs and widths. Finite laboratory-frame trajectory and action-word checks
corroborate the encoding and intervention maps. Records include leaving-code
