# Future equivalence is the minimal predictive refinement

Fix an F^q-invariant family, T=F^q, and R0=ker(P). The relation R_t identifies initial states with equal observed words P(s),...,P(T^t(s)); R_infinity identifies equal complete observed futures. It is the coarsest forward-invariant equivalence relation contained in R0. A present-only factor exists exactly when R0=R_infinity.

[Research023](../research/2026-09-08-history-lift-closure.md) exhaustively computes finite-ring history closure, while [Research025](../research/2026-09-08-fiber-visibility.md) identifies the stable predictive quotient. Finite partition refinement terminates, but a uniform temporal-depth or spatial-radius bound does not follow. On n=8,10,12,14,16, Rule106 has derivative depths 1,1,1,1,1 and block-parity depths 3,5,13,15,17; these are finite comparisons, not asymptotic theorems.

Forward observed-word partitions are not the internal state of an online suffix learner. The [shared synthesis](../research/2026-09-10-shared-closure-account.md) fixes this distinction and the common cadence. No adaptive-learning or depth-optimality result is implied.
