# Which re-interpretations of a represented dynamics are admissible, and what does each cost?

Open question of the [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../research/2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md) program. Complement conjugation, reflection, the local Z2 relabeling of rule fields, and change of ambient completion on a shared invariant family are the transformations the record has used so far. They are different operations with different reach, not one group. Each must be declared with the components it transports, its invertibility on the family, and its cost in touched sites, information, and locality. The [first audit](../research/2026-09-11-representation-invariants-audit.md) covers the two global ones; local recodings with a locality budget and completions are next.
