# Which re-interpretations of a represented dynamics are admissible, and what does each cost?

Partly answered question of the [Invariants Across Representation Contracts](../research/2026-09-10-representation-invariants-program.md) program. Complement conjugation, reflection, local relabeling, block recoding, lossy observation and change of ambient completion are different operations with different reach, not one group. Each must be declared with the components it transports, its invertibility on the family, and its cost in touched sites, information and locality.

The [first audit](../research/2026-09-11-representation-invariants-audit.md) covers the two global transformations. The completed [two-block recoding](../research/2026-09-11-higher-block-recoding.md) supplies one injective local alphabet-changing example with explicit storage and radius cost. The completed [second-lift comparison](../research/2026-09-11-second-lift-completion.md) supplies one bounded H128/H160 completion-change example on a shared invariant Rule32 family; fixed-depth fibers agree under local triangular recoding while literal correction tables can differ.

Broader local recodings, `k>2`, other completion families and budgets, and a general admissibility/cost taxonomy remain open. The completed examples are controls, not evidence that every representation change is cost-free or belongs to one algebraic family.
