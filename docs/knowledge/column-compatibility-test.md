# Exact column compatibility experiment

The completed frozen-protocol search tests 16,002 finite-height conditions
and all 256 elementary targets at cadences one, two, and three through a graph
covering arbitrary periodic column height. Independent Boolean truth sets and
direct return-path searches confirm the graph. Finite action and damage
tests retain their separate intervention and size bounds.

The resulting invariant families partly answer the global-compatibility
question. A dimension-general conveyor construction is a labeled deductive
follow-up. Full methods, code, and data are in the
[Research note](../research/2026-09-08-column-compatibility.md).
