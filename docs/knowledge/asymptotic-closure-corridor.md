# The asymptotic-closure corridor is broad under block parity

Under the fixed nonoverlapping block-2 parity observer at cadence two, the exact
future-equivalence tower gives a minimal predictive refinement sequence for
each finite periodic ECA ring.

A fresh-width test at \(n=16,18\) compared:

- Rule 30 as a chaotic-to-identity anchor;
- Rule 184 as a coarse transport anchor;
- Rules 54 and 110 as the core complex candidates.

Let

\[
R(n)=n-H_\infty(n)
\]

be the safe-forgetting reserve of the stable predictive quotient.

At both fresh widths,

\[
R_{30}(n)<R_{54}(n),R_{110}(n)<R_{184}(n).
\]

Numerically:

- \(n=16\): Rule 30 = 0.000244 bits, Rule 54 = 0.7441,
  Rule 110 = 0.5385, Rule 184 = 4.0045;
- \(n=18\): Rule 30 = 0.000130 bits, Rule 54 = 0.7724,
  Rule 110 = 0.6059, Rule 184 = 4.5020.

The minimal predictive-history depth also acquires new distinctions relative to
the previously observed width-14 baselines:

\[
h_{54}:5\to7,\qquad h_{110}:4\to6
\]

by width 18.

Thus, on this bounded observer/panel, the core complex rules keep requiring new
predictive distinctions as system size grows while their stable quotient does
not simply collapse to microscopic identity or remain as coarse as the
transport control.

This is an exact finite-domain finding, not an asymptotic theorem. It does not
prove unbounded history depth, convergence of the reserve, representation
independence, or a Class-IV classifier.

Source: [asymptotic closure scaling](../research/2026-10-07-asymptotic-closure-scaling.md).

## Revision, 2026-10-07: the corridor does not discriminate

An [independent census](../research/2026-10-07-asymptotic-closure-census-control.md)
of all 88 canonical representatives reproduced the values above and then ran
the control the original protocol stopped short of. 55 of 88 representatives
satisfy the reserve corridor at widths 16 and 18, and 41 of 88 also gain
history depth from width 14 to 18. Those 41 include 37 rules outside the
Class-IV set. The corridor is a matching variable under this observer, not a
discriminator, and Rule 62 nearly shadows Rule 110 on it.

asymptotic-closure corridor survives fresh scaling tests", described the
frozen gate and overstated what passing it means. The registry entry carried
