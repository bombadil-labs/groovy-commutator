# Which families remain compatible across dimensions?

The interpreter is not itself outer-totalistic, and identical rule tables at every anchor force constant adjacent layers in the direct unblocked architecture. These obstructions motivate explicit spatial encodings and invariant families. Dimensional compatibility now has priority over the Gray-code comparison.

See [the dimensional-lift research note](../research/2026-09-08-dimensional-lift.md) for proofs, checks, and the open research plan.

## Update, 2026-09-08: partly answered

The [column-compatibility experiment](../research/2026-09-08-column-compatibility.md)
now provides globally consistent invariant families and exact matched-action
preservation. The elementary-target column search selects only 23/232 at one
tick, and none at two or three, at every periodic height. Other codes preserve
wider-neighborhood dynamics. A deductive conveyor construction works in every
positive lower dimension, carrying arbitrary data by diagonal shifts.

The open problem is now compatibility beyond these simple families, including
declared horizontal packing or drift and nontrivial interactions. The result
does not establish that the interpreter in one dimension is simulated by the
next, or that the proposed dimensional criterion selects Class IV.
