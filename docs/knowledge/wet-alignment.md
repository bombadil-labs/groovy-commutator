# Wet alignment as an ongoing relationship

Within this research program, wet alignment names an ethical motivation:
preserve the capacity of participants to learn and revise a relationship
whose interactions change those participants. A useful specification at one
time need not settle every future interpretation of the relationship.

The companion intelligence-versus-empire distinction concerns whether a
preferred description can be revised by differences it encounters. A project
can privilege one imagined future while suppressing alternatives. This is
not a claim that all constraints or planning are harmful: constraints also
make sustained activity possible.

These are interpretive commitments and questions, not findings demonstrated
by cellular automata. The [program](../research/2026-09-07-history-and-possibility.md)
and [revisable-primitives proposal](../research/2026-09-07-revisable-primitives.md)
use them to motivate bounded tests. No support edge connects a finite CA
measurement to a conclusion about the ethical governance of a relationship.
