# The complement of a non-self-dual observation closes and is the residual class's only guaranteed member

For an elementary observation `ψ`, the rule `¬ψ = 255 − ψ` is closed under `ψ` with factor `ψ ∘ ¬` on the image, because its observed successor `ψ(¬ψ(S))` is a function of `ψ(S)`. For rules 32, 200 and 22 on rings 6 to 12, `¬ψ` neither commutes nor collapses, so it lies in the residual class `X` of closed rules with a non-constant factor that is not the rule. The residual class is not the observational-equivalence class of `¬ψ`: under rule 32, `X = {33, 171, 223, 241}` and each is equivalent to a commuter (223 to the identity); under rule 200, `X` has 17 rules in six equivalence classes, none containing a commuter, and 16 of them have a factor that is not radius-1 consistent on image words; under rule 22, `X = {233}` at rings 7 to 12 but `{223, 233}` at ring 6, the first ring-dependent closed set among the program's non-linear censuses. Observational equivalence itself is local: since `ψ ∘ F_r` has radius 2, two rules are equivalent on one ring of size at least 5 exactly when their 32 five-cell tables agree, and the census confirms this for observations 32, 200, 22 and 4 at every ring; under rule 4 rules 123, 232 and 251 form one class, which certifies the ninth unit's identity `π ∘ F_123 = π ∘ F_251` for every ring. Depth annex: under majority the record rules 106, 120, 169, 225 reach depth 25, 26, 28, 31 at rings 13 to 16; under rule 4 the record pair 62, 118 reaches 19, 19, 28, 25. Scope: three observations, rings 6 to 12, six annex rules at rings 13 to 16; the non-elementary factors under rule 200 are unexplained.
