# How useful can sound approximations remain when closure fails?

For a declared invariant source family and observation, the exact successor-set transformer retains every possible next observed field. A cheaper ordered representation can be sound while losing precision. The choice of order, allowed abstract objects, cost and precision metric remains part of the representation contract.

[The eight-cell worked example](../research/2026-09-10-successor-set-archive.md) compares exact successor relations with local product approximations. It establishes neither a general optimal approximation nor infinite-lattice soundness. Entropy bounds require a declared prior and matching observation weights. New substrates or observer comparisons require a separate frozen protocol.
