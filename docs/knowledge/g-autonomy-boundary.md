# Groovy autonomy depends on the source rule and domain

A derived field can have its own autonomous CA law. For every binary source
configuration on the integer line, Rule 32's original Groovy field obeys
G_32 E_32 = E_128 G_32. This is a nonconstant local factor, already proved in
the [local-correction-cap work](../research/2026-09-10-local-correction-caps.md).

Rule 110's G has no deterministic present-only factor on the full binary line
at cadence one. The aligned periodic source words 001 and 011 both give
G=010, but their next G words are 010 and 000 respectively. Locality transfers
this finite certificate to periodic infinite configurations. Even a nonlocal
function of the whole current G field cannot assign both required successors.
See the [exact result and certificate](../research/2026-09-22-rule110-g-autonomy.md).

This concerns original-source G, not a lifted descendant's native G, a
successive correction row, or same-law commutation. It does not exclude
autonomy on a declared invariant subfamily, with additional retained context,
or at a different cadence. Those contracts need their own tests. The witness
requires any autonomous refinement preserving G to distinguish its two sources;
it does not show that one extra bit is sufficient everywhere.
