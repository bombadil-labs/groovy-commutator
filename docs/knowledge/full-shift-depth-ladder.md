# The full-shift depth ladder separates from the ring ladder at every level above closure

For observations 232, 200, 22 and 4, every rule's full-shift refinement depth class `0, 1, 2, 3, ≥4` (from the certified closure sets, the full-shift depth-one and depth-two sets, and the sparse depth-three certificate on 65,536-vertex pair graphs) is tabulated against its rings-3-to-14 class from the exhaustive sets. The two agree exactly at class 0, closure, as the thirteenth unit certified, and differ at every level above: under 232 sixteen rules with ring class 1 have full-shift class 2 or at least 4, and twelve with ring class 2 have full-shift class 3. Of the 24 rules with depth at most one on every ring but at least three on the full shift, 20 have full-shift depth at least four (all eight under 232, all four under 200, eight of twelve under 22) and only four have depth exactly three. The rings-3-to-14 depth-three gap is nonempty under all four observations (8, 11, 8, 10 rules). Full-shift depth-three sets: 198, 181, 234, 106 rules; each is the conjugate transport of the set for the conjugate observation, and observation 32's sets are the transport of 4's by the identity `F_32 = F_4∘¬`.
