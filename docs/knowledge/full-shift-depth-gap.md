# Full-shift depth two is decided by sparse reachability, the periodic depth gap recurs at depth two, and it can be deep

For an observation `ψ` and rule `r`, pairs agreeing on `ψ`, `ψ ∘ F_r` and `ψ ∘ F_r²` are walks in a 4096-vertex pair graph on six-cell pair blocks, and `h_* ≤ 2` fails on the full shift exactly when a violating nine-cell block is bi-infinitely extendable: its start reachable from a cycle and its end reaching a cycle. This is one reachability problem per pair, so full-shift depth at most two is decided for all eight observations and all 256 rules without matrix powers (178, 97, 97, 172, 186, 256, 236, 222 rules under 232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 102, 90, 150). Against exhaustive depth two on rings 3 to 14, the gap "depth at most two at every tested ring but not on the full shift" is nonempty for every observation except parity, including 4, 32, 90 and 150, which had no depth-one gap. Of the fourteenth unit's 54 pairs with depth at most one at every ring but not on the full shift, 24 (under 232, 200 and 22) also fall outside full-shift depth two, so a rule can need one step of observed history on every ring and at least three on the line. Each of the 54 is now an explicit eventually periodic pair of configurations, periodic on both sides of a finite defect. Scope: eight observations, full-shift depth at most two only; no ring certificate at depth two, so the gaps are statements about rings 3 to 14.
