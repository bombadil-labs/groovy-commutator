# Does three-dimensional space support a distinctive representation?

**Proposed, not tested.** On 2026-09-08, Myk reported that parallel Wet Math
work suggests a kind of representation possible specifically in three
dimensions, unavailable in both lower and higher dimensions. The precise
argument and represented object have not yet been supplied to this research
record. No particular topological or dynamical mechanism is assumed.

To test the proposal, specify what dimension measures, the represented
relation, allowable embeddings and transformations, and the property required
to survive them. Storing lower-dimensional data with an added constant
coordinate is possible; a three-only property needs a more selective
operational or structural requirement. Fix that requirement before comparing
dimensions two, three, and four.

The [current block experiment](../research/2026-09-08-block-compatibility.md)
does not test this hypothesis. Its three-row block occupies two-dimensional
space. Likewise, the dimensional conveyor carries data in every positive
lower dimension and supplies no evidence of a three-dimensional exception.
The next step is to recover the squad's exact formulation and derive a
comparison that could falsify it.
