# Does a source rule confer a susceptibility to the complex phenotype on its refinements?

The handoff's conjecture defines a refinement depth `d_4(F)` as the least
added freedom under which a source-faithful refinement of `F` enters the
selective-persistence × sustained-spreading regime. As stated it is not well
posed: the phenotype is observer-relative and "source-faithful without
independent information" has no definition, while the trivial bolt-on lemma
(product with an unrelated automaton) makes every unrestricted version empty.
It becomes well posed inside an explicit finite family. The first such
family is the height-one strip fiber of a reflection-symmetric ECA (4096
outer-totalistic rules, no bolt-on freedom, the observation contract fixed by
the strip). The frozen fiber census asks whether the phenotype's distribution
over the fiber depends on the base rule; rule 110 has no fiber in this family.
