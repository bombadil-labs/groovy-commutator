on periodic rings of 6, 9, and 11 cells; 66 distinct pairs from a 12-rule

cases. Six witnesses were selected by the frozen lexicographic procedure
and independently replayed with the vectorized engine. Transition, set/word,
zero-horizon, and identity/reset controls passed.
