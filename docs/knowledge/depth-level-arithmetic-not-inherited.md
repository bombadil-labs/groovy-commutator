# Ring windows and eventual periods are properties of (observation, depth level), not of depth: at depth three the lying window moves to 200 and the period under 22 collapses from 12 to 1

Refinement depth at most three is now decided at **every** ring size for all 256 rules under 232, 4, 32, 200, 22, 90, 150 by strong components of the 65,536-vertex depth-three pair graph, with no matrix power taken: a rule has depth at most `h` at every ring iff its pair graph has no violating three-edge walk with both ends inside one strong component (the protocol's Lemma A, which turns the eighteenth unit's post hoc identity into a theorem at every depth). The all-ring depth-three sets have sizes 206, 116, 116, 187, 242, 254, 246 and the all-ring gaps against the full shift 8, 10, 10, 6, 8, 4, 0 rules, so the gap is nonempty under exactly six observations: those rules have depth at most three on every finite periodic ring `n ≥ 4` and full-shift depth at least four, meaning the collection of all periodic-ring tests taken together does not decide the full-shift depth-three property, while each individual ring's membership is decided.

The informative content is what did **not** carry up from depth two. At depth two the one observation whose rings-3-to-14 window overstated the all-ring gap was 22, by twelve rules leaving at ring 24. At depth three 22's window is **exact**, and the single observation whose window overstates is **200**: five rules — 41, 97, 169, 225, 233 — keep depth at most three on every ring 3 to 14 and leave `D³_200(n)` at ring 16, each witnessed in one strong component of size 40 and period 1. The eventual periods move just as unpredictably: `P³_ψ = 1, 2, 2, 1, 1, 2, 6` against the certified depth-two 1, 2, 2, 1, 12, 4, 6. Under 22 the period collapses from 12 to 1, every rule with a closed violation being eventually out; under 90 it halves from 4 to 2; under 4 and 32 it stays at 2 but on an entirely different population — rule 50, the sole depth-two oscillator under 4, is trivially in at depth three, while 22, 54 and 179 oscillate instead; only 232, 200 and 150 keep their depth-two values. So a depth-`h` ring window or period is not evidence about depth `h + 1` even under the same observation, and such claims must carry their level. This extends the seventeenth unit's finding — that whether periodic configurations decide a level is a property of the pair (observation, level) — from the qualitative gap to the quantitative invariants, the first failing ring and the eventual period.

Also observed, exactly and post hoc on one observation: under 200 the five rules that survive to ring 16 are precisely the eventually-out rules whose carrying component contains no diagonal vertex, and each carries exactly one such component; the two other non-diagonal rules (33, 161) carry a second, size-46 component that fires earlier and pulls their first failure back to ring 10. A failure that cannot be witnessed by a configuration pair agreeing on an eight-cell block is the failure a short ring cannot see. This is the same property whose absence failed the unit's anchoring bet under 200 and 232, so one structural fact explains both a held and a failed prediction.
