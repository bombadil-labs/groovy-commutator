# Outer-totalistic dimensional encoding

A ternary count address selects a table position; the center bit selects one of the two layers. This is an exact local encoding and an explicit interpreter, with a declared transverse axis and digit order. Global overlapping patches and persistence of programs require additional conditions.

See [the dimensional-lift research note](../research/2026-09-08-dimensional-lift.md) for proofs, checks, and the open research plan.
