# Under a non-linear observation, a rule can close with a factor that is neither constant nor the rule itself

Under isolated-cell detection (rule 4 applied once) on rings 6 to 12, exactly 33 rules close, the same at every ring: 9 exact commuters `{0, 4, 42, 112, 170, 200, 204, 232, 240}`, 24 collapse rules whose observed successor is always the all-zeros field, and two rules, 123 and 251, that neither commute nor collapse: their induced factor on the image is rule 32 restricted to image words, non-constant and different from the rule. Rule 251 is `¬F_4`, the complement of the observation, so its observed successor is determined by the observation by construction. Rule 123 is not a function of the observation (`0ⁿ` and `1ⁿ` share the observation but map to `1ⁿ` and `0ⁿ`); on the tested rings its observed successor agrees with rule 251's on every state, an identity with no proof beyond ring 12. So the commute-or-collapse shape found under majority does not generalize. Under majority this class could not appear because `¬F_232 = F_23` commutes with majority, which is self-dual. Complement conjugation is covariant, not invariant: the census transports exactly to observation 223 under `r ↦ r̃`, while the two closed sets differ. The affine rules beyond constants, identity, shifts and their complements are not closed. See the [isolated-cell note](../research/2026-09-11-isolated-cell.md). Scope: one observation, rings 6 to 12; nothing is claimed about the closed-set structure under other non-linear observations.
