# A rule can close under an observation by being a function of the observation itself

Under isolated-cell detection (rule 4 applied once) on rings 6 to 12, exactly 33 rules close, the same at every ring: 9 exact commuters `{0, 4, 42, 112, 170, 200, 204, 232, 240}`, 24 collapse rules whose observed successor is always the all-zeros field, and two rules, 123 and 251, that neither commute nor collapse. Rule 251 is `¬F_4`, the complement of the observation, so its observed successor is a function of the observation by construction; its factor on the image is rule 32 restricted to image words. Rule 123 differs from 251 only on the window `111` and has the same observed successor on every tested state. So closure under a non-linear observation has at least three algebraic routes: commutation, collapse, and factoring through the observation. Under majority the third route coincided with commutation because `¬F_232` is self-dual. Complement conjugation is covariant, not invariant: the census transports exactly to observation 223 under `r ↦ r̃`, while the two closed sets differ. The affine rules beyond constants, identity, shifts and their complements are not closed. See the [isolated-cell note](../research/2026-09-11-isolated-cell.md). Scope: one observation, rings 6 to 12; that three routes exhaust closure elsewhere is not claimed.
