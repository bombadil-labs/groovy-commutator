# Does strong history repair identify Class IV?

## Former proposal

Strong improvement from adding history might identify Class IV behavior. This entry records that simple repair-only shortcut, rather than every possible criterion combining compression, closure, and memory.

## Why it fails

Rule 30 also shows very strong repair under derivative observation. Changing the observation changes the comparison. The result refutes the simple discriminating claim in the tested setting; it does not establish a replacement classifier or rule out more carefully specified future criteria.
