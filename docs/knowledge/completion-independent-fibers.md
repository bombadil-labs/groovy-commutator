# Completion changes coordinates without changing the retained whole-field fibers

Suppose local total laws H and H' on the same GF(2)^k configuration space agree on a shared invariant family B. Their derivative observations I XOR H and I XOR H' agree on B, and their finite observed-future tuples coincide there.

The triangular coordinate theorem supplies K_h^{H'}=(T_h^{H'})^{-1} T_h^H K_h^H on B. At each fixed finite depth this is an invertible local recoding. Whole-field fibers and existence of a whole-field autonomous factor are therefore completion-independent. Literal correction maps, local patch distinctions and radius/cost budgets may differ.

[The protocol checkpoint](../research/2026-09-10-second-lift-completion-protocol.md) records the consequence before the Rule32 second-lift census. The full ambient comparison has different source dynamics and is outside the shared-family premise. This is an analytic result, not a completed numerical experiment.
