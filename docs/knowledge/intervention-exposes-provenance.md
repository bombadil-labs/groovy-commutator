# An action can expose hidden provenance differences

The all-zero six-cell panel admits 128 fixed rules that preserve zero forever, yet a single cell flip gives 38 distinct four-step continuations. Across width-eight panels after six observed steps, future ambiguity changes from 96 panels naturally to 4,134 after the flip. Other panels lose alternatives after the intervention; no universal monotonicity is claimed.
