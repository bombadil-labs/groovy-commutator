# Binary lift census and collision diagnosis

Status: completed local experiment series; independent Gate 2 review pending. Myk approved the skipped Gate 1 exception, recorded in the [retrospective methods](../research/protocols/binary-lift-retrospective-record-20260914.md).

The series accounts for two complete 24,576-recipe first-floor censuses, one selected 3D path for each of 256 rules, and every faithful recipe for all 36 first-floor G holdouts. It preserves the older 113-path 4D population separately. See the [construction](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-family.md), [recursion](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-recursion.md), and [obstruction](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-g-obstructions.md) records.

Exact coverage over the tested finite domains is distinguished from adaptive recipe selection, targeted independent implementations within the author session, and independent-agent review. The result does not close the general recursive-G problem.
