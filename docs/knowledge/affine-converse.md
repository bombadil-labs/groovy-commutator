# Does a constant commutator imply affine evolution?

## Former claim

The project once stated an equivalence between affine evolution and a constant self-commutator. The reverse direction would infer affine evolution from a constant commutator.

## Counterexample and correction

Rules 4 and 200 are nonlinear yet have identically zero self-commutators. Retain the affine-to-constant implication and reject the equivalence.
