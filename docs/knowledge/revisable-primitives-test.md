# Compare fixed and revisable compiled primitives

**Planned; not run.** Compare systems with the same starting operations,
tasks, and budgets. Successful constructions may become reusable primitives.
One policy freezes those constructions; another permits inspection,
decomposition, and replacement. Include an uncompiled baseline and a control
that permits replacement without exposing the internal construction.

Construction, storage, execution, inspection, and revision must all have
explicit costs. Neither arm receives a free superset of actions. Specify an
environmental change and held-out capability tests before comparing outcomes.

The [plan](../research/2026-09-07-revisable-primitives.md) records the fairness
problems that must be resolved before freezing a protocol. Evidence from the
fixed-action repertoire experiment does not predict which arm will win.
