# Sequential native binary lifts through 6D on a finite family

Status: exploratory, bounded computation awaiting final independent Gate 2.

Six published recipe paths (90, 54, 110, 157, 171, 233) retain autonomous native evolution and local immediate-parent recovery through 6D on the complete width-seven source family. Composed decoding recovers the original source. Every parent rule is completed deterministically and fixed before its child is constructed.

The [research note](../research/2026-09-14-sequential-lift-6d-pilot.md) states the exact domain and measurements. One-step identities on this complete invariant family imply the same correspondence for every time within that family.

These are newly synthesized finite-family tables; archived full-shift and G constraints are not retained. The recipe is explicit metadata in the finite current-system object. This is neither a bare-table generator for arbitrary C nor a proof of all-input or all-dimension generality. Native radius remains two; source-recovery radius under decoder composition may grow.

The result motivates a stronger local invariant or domain-enlargement test. Failure on a larger domain would refine, not erase, this finite-family finding.
