# Equal immediate costs can conceal different future costs

With rules 4/30, inherited ABBB, and target AAAB, keeping the macro or patching
to ABAB both cost eight for the current one-job block under dispatch price
one and a free trace. A lexicographic tie rule patches; a stay-on-tie rule
keeps the current construction. The returning old task costs thirteen versus
ten, respectively.

The [returning-task experiment](../research/2026-09-07-returning-tasks.md)
predeclared both tie rules. The witness shows that equal present objective
values can leave different future costs. At the primary four-job settings,
the two revision tie rules have equal costs; the effect belongs to the
declared one-job sensitivity, not every tested condition.
