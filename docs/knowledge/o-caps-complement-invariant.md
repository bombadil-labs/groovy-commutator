# O-coordinate caps are complement-invariant; K caps shift by at most two

Over all 256 rules, depths `h ≤ 2`, radii `R ≤ 4`: the pass/fail table of O-coordinate local caps is identical for a rule and its complement-conjugate at every radius, because `B_j = D∘F^j` transforms as a difference and every O shift is zero. K-coordinate caps shift by 0, 1 or 2 (never above `h + 1`; observed maximum 2 at `h = 2`, in the pairs 132↔222 and 160↔250). No cell is half-decided: the transformation never creates or destroys a cap within the budget. Reflection leaves both tables identical. Post hoc: the 30 rules with a depth-0 cap of either kind are exactly the rules whose derivative observation closes on rings `n ≤ 10`. See the [shift census](../research/2026-09-11-cap-shift-census.md). Scope: `R ≤ 4`, one transformation.
