# Recurrence plus disturbance spreading separates the core ECA families in fresh tests

A finite predictor selects nonzero normalized spacetime-recurrence error below 1/2 together with a disturbance-diameter doubling exponent above 1/2. Cutoffs were selected after discovery, then held fixed. Rules 54/110 each pass twelve fresh runs; none of 84 undisputed negative representatives passes any of their 1,008 runs. Disputed 106 passes 4/12. Only two positive symmetry families are represented, and the held-out units are input conditions, not unseen rules.

The two clauses address different controls: in these sampled runs, 73 retains recurrence but shows little late-time diameter growth; chaotic controls usually spread influence without comparable recurrence. This is a useful ECA baseline, not an established invariant Class IV definition.

A wider-radius right-permutive family preserves iid spatial slices yet can pass the same finite score, including through 4096-step disturbance continuations. Thus selection does not establish an ordered spatial background. The wider rules have no assigned Wolfram labels. Empty-interval tails have a conditional invariance proof but are also confounded by absorption mixtures.
