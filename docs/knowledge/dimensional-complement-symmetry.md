# The dimensional interpreter respects complement and reflection

The proof uses address(3^d−1−n) = −address(n) and the exchange of the two rule layers when the center complements. This symmetry holds on all configurations, including uniform fixed states; it does not identify a special dynamical class or establish a cross-dimensional projection.
