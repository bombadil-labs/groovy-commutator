# Closure at every ring is decided by a sixteen-vertex pair graph, and the audited closed sets are constant from ring 7

For an observation `ψ`, pairs of ring configurations with equal observations are the closed walks of length `n` in the pair graph `G_ψ` (vertices two-cell pair blocks, edges three-cell pair blocks on which `ψ` agrees). A rule fails closure at ring `n ≥ 4` exactly when a five-cell pair block with disagreeing observed successors, a violating three-edge walk, has a return walk of length `n − 3`; boolean powers of the adjacency matrix repeat after `k` steps with period `p`, so the closed sets are periodic in `n` from `k + 3` and the list `C_ψ(4), …, C_ψ(k+p+2)` decides every ring. Divisibility follows separately: a closure lost at ring `n` is lost at every multiple. For observations 232, 4, 32, 200, 22 and 102 the closed set is the same at every ring from 7 on and equals the closure on the full shift (sizes 22, 33, 33, 42, 11, 32); rule 223 under 22 closes at ring 6 and nowhere else; rings 3 to 5 carry many more closures, which exist only because the ring fits inside the criterion's window. The linear observations 90 and 150 never settle: 90 closes the 32 constant-complement-response rules on odd rings and the 16 affine rules on even rings, 150 all 256 rules off multiples of 3 and the 16 affine rules on them, periods 2 and 3, certified for every ring. Observation 32 is observation 4 of the complemented state, so their censuses are conjugate (post hoc). See the [ring-closure-certificate note](../research/2026-09-11-ring-closure-certificate.md). Scope: eight observations, closure only; depth and factor radius are not certified beyond the rings earlier units computed.
