# Separated data planes form a dimensional conveyor

Under the same ternary interpreter in every positive lower dimension, an
arbitrary data plane separated from its repeats by zero planes moves one
transverse step and one step along every spatial axis per tick. With period
m>=2, it returns to its encoded phase after m ticks, carrying a diagonal
shift of the original data. The local proof is dimension-general; finite
checks cover lower dimensions one, two, and three.

The construction preserves the corresponding logical flips at sampled phases.
It is an exact transport family, not a demonstration of interacting computation
or recursive simulation of one interpreter by the next. This was a deductive
follow-up to the [column search](../research/2026-09-08-column-compatibility.md).
