# A logical action can depend on physical coordination

For the six-cell Rule-90 encoding, flipping its two variable cells in every
vertical period is a matched logical flip. Its response is independent of
background at every even fine time, by the encoding identity and XOR linearity.

The individual cell flips do not have that property: at fine tick four each
has 200 distinct responses across the 512 causal backgrounds. Their joint
response is not the XOR of their separate responses; the interaction term is
nonzero on every background. Together they nevertheless give one exact
logical response.

Confining the same two-cell action to one block instead produces 200 distinct
responses at tick four and remains outside the global encoding. The action
map includes its vertical extent. This is scoped to the fixed interpreter,
encoding, and declared actions, not a universal claim about cooperation.
See [the experiment](../research/2026-09-08-encoded-defects.md).
