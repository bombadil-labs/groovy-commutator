# Under block majority a rule closes by commuting with the observation or by collapsing it

Three-cell majority (rule 232) applied once as an observation is non-linear, non-injective and non-surjective. On rings 6 to 12 exactly 22 rules close, the same set at every ring: the 14 rules that commute exactly with rule 232, `{0, 4, 15, 23, 32, 51, 85, 170, 204, 223, 232, 240, 251, 255}`, whose factor on the image is the rule itself, and 8 rules, `{2, 8, 16, 64, 191, 239, 247, 253}`, whose outputs never contain two adjacent equal cells of one kind, so the observed successor is a constant field and the factor is constant. The affine rules other than constants, identity, shifts and their complements are not closed, witnessed by the all-zeros state and one isolated cell, which share the observation and have different observed successors. Neither the parity closed set nor the majority closed set contains the other. The refinement depth `h_*` is 0 exactly on the closed set, reaches 2, 4, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 16 at rings 6 to 12 (rules 106, 120, 169, 225 at ring 12), and changes with the ring for 88 rules. All tables are complement- and reflection-invariant. See the [block majority note](../research/2026-09-11-block-majority.md). Scope: one non-linear observation, rings 6 to 12; the depth growth is a census fact without proof.
