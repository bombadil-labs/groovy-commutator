# Can one binary lift grammar preserve G indefinitely for every ECA?

Status: open.

The [current family](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-family.md) realizes every ECA faithfully, and its [selected repeat](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-recursion.md) preserves native evolution and both recovery gates through 3D. G remains incomplete: 36 first-floor gaps and 14 eligible rules with no saved 3D G path.

The [probe diagnosis](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-g-obstructions.md) identifies conditions a recursive invariant must preserve: derivative-neighborhood sufficiency and compatibility with native evolution, in addition to recovery. The next unrun experiment filters other two-input Q functions at the same offsets against exact contradictory pairs. Witness separation is necessary, not sufficient. Alphabet, native radius, preparation and independent-information costs must remain explicit.
