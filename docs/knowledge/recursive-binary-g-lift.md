# Which meaning of recursive G does the binary lift preserve?

Status: the historical native P/D-carrier construction remains incomplete;
completion-independent ancestral G transport is proved by a later theorem.
Updated 2026-09-22. The DT2/complement extension carries Gate 2 acceptance
(2026-09-14, Claude/Fable, PR #233).

The [extended family](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-complements.md) supplies
native evolution, source recovery and centered G at the first floor for all
256 ECA sources. The earlier [3D
repeat](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-recursion.md) remains 256 faithful
and 206 best-known G paths; the new first floors were not recursively tested.
The original 36 first-floor prerequisites were repaired, but the 14 old
eligible 3D gaps and 36 new carriers require separate recursive accounting
under that historical grammar.

The [probe diagnosis](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-g-obstructions.md) and [DT2 comparison](../research/2026-09-14-binary-lift-dt2.md) identify the prospective invariant: retain derivative-neighborhood sufficiency, source/parent recovery and compatibility between native and probe outputs. Row polarity can repair overlap without changing the probe. Parent completion constraints and preparation radius must remain explicit.

Original G cannot be universally carried by the unchanged P/D contract when
faithful source fixed points with `E_r(0)=1` are included; the complement note
proves that obstruction. Centered G is therefore the stated universal target
of this historical construction, not a relabeling of original G.

The later [affine-oriented lift
theorem](../research/2026-09-17-affine-oriented-lift-theorem.md) changes the
global status without completing this census. It proves exact lifting for
arbitrary binary finite-memory parents and transports ancestral source G (and
the horizon commutators `K_t`) completion-independently through every finite
depth using two marked point beams. It does **not** make the descendant rule's
native cellwise G completion-independent. The remaining one-beam typed bundle
asks whether those two beams compress to one marked point frame plus a locally
defined vector sector. The [boundary
note](../research/2026-09-22-recursive-g-boundary.md) separates these objects.
Both the old carrier census and the one-beam compression are dormant absent a
consumer.
