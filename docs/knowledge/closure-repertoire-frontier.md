# Breaking closure cheaply and maximizing hidden futures are different objectives

[Research026](../research/2026-09-08-possibility-frontier.md) defines future repertoire as V_infinity=H(C_infinity|P(S)), the latent term of the declared finite-state information budget. It compares minimum forgetting among closure-breaking observations with maximum repertoire. The observation also defines what future differences count, so changing it changes the target semantics.

On the exhaustive n=12 static family of identity, constant and two-/three-cell Boolean block observations, 247 of 256 ECA rules have a tested closure breaker. For 222 of those 247, no tested observer jointly attains the two optima. Block size also fixes cadence q=2 or3; the comparison is over those declared observation/cadence pairs. Identity and constant observation both have zero repertoire, for different reasons.

This is an exact bounded comparison under uniform initial microstates. Repertoire is neither physical indeterminism nor the capacity of an action channel. Class-label interpretations and the separate relational derivative comparison retain their original qualifications; no universal observer or optimization principle is established.
