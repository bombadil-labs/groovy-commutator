# What becomes possible when an organization dissolves?

Rearranging intact units differs from breaking the organization that makes
those units persist. The plasma and death vocabulary in the
[wider program](../research/2026-09-07-history-and-possibility.md) motivates
this distinction; it is not evidence for a universal physical cycle.

A test needs to define the tracked organization, its continuity criterion,
and its substrate before intervention. Loss of the organization, persistence
of components or effects, and formation of a new organization are separate
outcomes. New organization is not guaranteed, and releasing one constraint
may destroy another capability.

This question is open. The existing repertoire probe does not define or
measure organizational survival. A future study should compare intact-unit
rearrangement with decomposition at matched resources and observe capabilities
at both scales.
